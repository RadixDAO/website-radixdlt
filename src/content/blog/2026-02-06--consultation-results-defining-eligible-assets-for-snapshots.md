<p>We have completed the consultation to define the assets that represent valid voting power in <a href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/consultation-defining-eligible-assets-for-snapshots">Radix Consultation Snapshots</a>.</p><p>As we transition to the community-led model, clarity is essential. There cannot have ambiguity about who has a voice in consultations. We asked you to input which assets should count, setting a threshold of 60% acceptance for inclusion.</p><p>With over 1.4 Billion XRD participating from 743 accounts, the community has drawn a clear line.</p><h3><strong>The Results</strong></h3><p>The community voted to INCLUDE the following assets (meeting the 60% threshold):</p><ul><li>XRD Staked on Top 100 Validators: 79% Support</li><li>XRD Staked on Any Validator: 69% Support</li><li>Liquid XRD held in Accounts: 69% Support</li><li>XRD in Supported DEX Pools*: 60% Support</li></ul><p>‍</p><p>*These will be the pools supported in Radix Rewards S1 for now.</p><p>The community voted to EXCLUDE the following assets (failing the threshold):</p><ul><li>Time-Locked XRD: 44% Support</li><li>Lent XRD: 28% Support</li></ul><p>‍</p><h3><strong>The New Standard</strong></h3><p>Future governance snapshots and consultations will calculate voting weight based on the aggregate sum of:</p><ol><li>Staked XRD (All validators)</li><li>Liquid XRD</li><li>XRD/LSUs in supported DEX Pools</li></ol><p>We will update the community when necessary changes to <a href="http://consultation.radixdlt.com">consultation.radixdlt.com</a> have been made so snapshots reflect this new standard.&nbsp;</p><p>Thank you to the 743 participants who participated.</p>
