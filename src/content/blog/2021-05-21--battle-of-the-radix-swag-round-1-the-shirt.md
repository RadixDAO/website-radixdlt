<p id="">So it begins, the<strong id=""> Battle of the Radix Swag contest</strong>. Drum roll. Cue the epic music!</p><p id="">Over the past few months, we have seen many in the community ask about how they could get some Radix-style apparel. At the same time, we have seen some incredible Radix-themed art being produced by the community. Seems a good fit, right? So, now we are ready to launch the <strong id="">new Radix store, </strong>we want to start things off with a community competition to help support the creative ideas in the community!<strong id=""> </strong>After all, who better than Radix enthusiasts (like you!) to create the designs that you’d love to wear, and get them featured on products sold in the store!&nbsp;</p><h3 id="">The first contest: Design a T-shirt or a Hoodie</h3><p id="">There’s a lot of talent in the Radix community and we want to showcase that brilliance through our swag. Details of the competition are below:</p><ul id=""><li id="">The contest will open on 21st May</li><li id="">Submissions close on 4th June at 23:59 UTC</li><li id="">The winning entry will have their designed product featured in the Radix store, 3,850 of eXRD*, and receive a Radix swag package</li><li id="">Two runners-up will also be selected and will receive 1925 of eXRD* each and receive a Radix swag package</li><li id="">Winners will be selected on 11th June 2021 at 23:59 UTC</li><li id="">You can make as many submissions as you want</li><li id="">All entries are subject to the competition <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/competition-terms">terms and conditions</a><br></li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id="">How to Enter:&nbsp;</h3><ol id=""><li id="">Create a T-shirt or Hoodie design in PNG or JPEG format with at least 150 DPI. For additional guidelines, read <a id="" href="https://www.printful.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-to-prepare-the-perfect-printfile/">this article</a>. Reference templates and print sizes can be found <a id="" href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1EwjUfifPidIBFGNKwl7O8rUZTM4qQVYN">here</a>.&nbsp;</li><li id="">Follow <a id="" href="https://twitter.com/radixdlt">@radixdlt </a>on Twitter, and post your submission with the hashtag #RadixSwag and tag <a id="" href="http://@radixdlt">@radixdlt</a>.&nbsp;</li><li id="">Post your submission also on the Discord #swag-contest channel <a id="" href="https://discord.gg/uMrdh6YM">here</a><strong id="">.</strong></li><li id="">Use <a id="" href="https://forms.gle/gjgZxBzCoBmeb8yQ7">this form</a> to submit your entry for review, including a link to the tweet and discord post.</li></ol><p id="">Submissions will be evaluated based on their creativity, originality, community response (likes, comments, etc.) and aesthetic quality by 3 judges selected by the Radix team.&nbsp;<br></p><p id="">Let’s go, get your designs in! We look forward to seeing your creativity, and can’t wait to see what you create!&nbsp;<br></p><h6 id=""><strong id="">*eXRD prizes may require completion of KYC checks and are subject to the </strong><a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/competition-terms"><strong id="">terms and conditions</strong></a><strong id=""> of the competition.</strong><br></h6>
