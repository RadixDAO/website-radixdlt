<p id="">Project Ignition is a $10M liquidity initiative for the <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/full-stack">Radix Network</a> designed to enhance liquidity for USDC, USDT, wBTC and ETH. Project Ignition matches each dollar Liquidity Providers (LPs) provide with an equal amount of XRD, effectively doubling the liquidity provided. LPs receive up to 20% of the provided value upfront in XRD and can claim trading fees while receiving protection from impermanent loss.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Project Ignition will be going live on March 14th, 2024, and will be integrated with leading Radix DEXs <a id="" href="http://ociswap.com">Ociswap</a>, <a href="http://caviarnine.com">CaviarNine</a>, and <a href="http://defiplaza.net">DefiPlaza</a>, which will serve as the gateway for users to access the Ignition incentives via specific liquidity pools. <br><br>Once liquidity is provided via one of the DEX front-ends, users will immediately receive an unlocked payment in XRD of up to 20% of the value of the tokens they provide as liquidity, depending on the lock-up period of the liquidity (9-12 months.)</p><p id="">As the user in Project Ignition is only providing liquidity on the wBTC/USDC/USDT or ETH side, the user is 100% protected from any XRD impermanent loss.</p><p id="">If the asset the liquidity provider has provided (wBTC/USDC/USDT/ETH) outperforms the RADIX token, the liquidity provider receives a value guarantee for up to a 4x outperformance of the asset provided, and then a sliding amount of asset value protection beyond a 4x change. See the <a id="" href="http://www.radixdlt.com/project-ignition-details">Project Ignition Details document</a> for a detailed breakdown of the IL protection.&nbsp;</p><p id="">To participate in the liquidity campaign, you must first bridge assets into the Radix ecosystem via Instabridge.
