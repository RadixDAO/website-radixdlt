<p id=""><a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radix-rewards-season-1-is-live">Radix Rewards</a>, the 100m+ XRD onchain incentive campaign, is well into the first full season. Since starting, we’ve seen Radix transactions and DEX volume over double compared to the previous period, and drive significant amounts of bridged assets across <a href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/hyperlane-is-live">Hyperlane</a>.</p><p id="">When the program began, we also announced a retrospective Season 0 to reward those who supported Radix before incentives started. Season 0 gives eligible users a bonus of up to 20 percent on everything they earn in Season 1.</p><p id="">The calculation period for that bonus is coming soon, and we need to finalize who is eligible.&nbsp;</p><p id="">The window to qualify for Season 0 closes at 00:00 UTC on <strong id="">December 17, 2025</strong>. To receive the bonus, you must register for Radix Rewards and link every account you want included before the cutoff.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">How the Season 0 bonus works</strong></h3><p id=""><a href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radix-rewards-season-0-turning-past-support-into-future-gains">Season 0</a> was designed to recognize long term supporters of the network. To calculate the bonus for Season 1, we use your holdings of XRD, LSUs, and other tracked pools across the full 52 week period before September 1, 2025. This data is then used to determine the extra percentage you will earn in Season 1.</p><p id="">The calculation will use a weekly time weighted average. This reflects sustained support, smooths out short term spikes, and rewards consistent participation. Every eligible account earns points each week based on its TWA position.</p><p id="">Your Season 0 bonus is based on your total points. It begins at 1 percent for accounts in the bottom 5 percent of the TWA distribution and increases proportionally up to 20 percent for those in the top 5 percent. The higher your position, the higher your multiplier on all Season 1 rewards.</p><p id="">The Season 0 bonus is extra. It does not reduce the Season 1 pool, and it does not lower anyone’s Season 1 earnings.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">What you need to do now</strong></h3><p id="">If you have not yet registered for Radix Rewards, or if you have additional wallets that should be counted, now is the time to link them. Only accounts connected by December 17 will be included in the Season 0 calculation.</p><p id="">To do so, visit incentives.radixdlt.com.&nbsp;</p><p id="">‍</p>
