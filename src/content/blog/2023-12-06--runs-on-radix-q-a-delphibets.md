<p id="">In the fast-paced world of DeFi, DELPHIBETS emerges as the first Prediction Protocol built on Radix. This protocol is set to improve what is currently on offer by creating a user-centric, and seamless experience.&nbsp;</p><p id="">This article will delve into the details of what DELPHIBETS is, what it allows users to do, and the specific problems it solves in the prediction market.</p><h2 id="">What is DELPHIBETS?</h2><p id="">DELPHIBETS is a decentralized prediction protocol that enables users to predict on a wide range of future events, such as elections, sporting events, and even what planet humans will land on next.&nbsp;</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1600px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/6570924aaf00cd38f376c8e7_v5FLkMWdsjqdI9jrZA7zago1xKk8ZRou2rhRhCe8VJvbq1BjSOcMiBfwpFSOIpshSb6y-9-MEOMcNarfxZ3wE1C-Llx7-f0BZf4w5U61e7KlAsXT4hiQi3bOcfDm_NrzBM-k99fzBcoi21hIDelL8Gc.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">Unlike traditional, centralized betting platforms *DELPHIBETS offers a democratized betting environment. It employs complex automated market maker (AMM) and parimutuel mechanisms, along with Peer-2-Peer (P2P) betting, to ensure a dynamic, responsive, and frictionless user experience.</p><h2 id="">What Can Users Do on DELPHIBETS?</h2><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1600px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/6570924abc4459c133a001b4_XYM1OpbZIndTTmcoctVnXUU2z1bByaSxE5PMjepmGo0yOFJ5tX8Iq23A_9bAV2yO3dgWmZpDFb_y3V3IrSPHOPzMfV3yy0h_ev7XyYLa_I5bnIh-0KjtB4-r4UxgWTIcM_usLUQBmBelyTUSZagvwtY.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">Users can engage in Peer-to-Peer betting, where they can directly interact with others looking to take the opposing view. Peer-to-peer betting allows everyone to set their own conditions and odds.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="">Types of Pool-Based Betting on DELPHIBETS</h3><p id=""><strong id="">Parimutuel Pool Bets:</strong> In this variant, all bets are pooled together, and the odds are set based on the total wagers placed on each possible outcome. Once the event concludes, the winnings are distributed among those who bet on the winning outcome. This approach aligns closely with traditional pool-based betting systems but offers the added benefit of a decentralized, transparent platform.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Uniswap-like AMM Approach:</strong> Taking a page from automated market makers like Uniswap, DELPHIBETS introduces a more dynamic form of pool-based betting. In this model, outcome shares are minted and can be redeemed by bettors who wager on the winning outcome. What's more, users have the flexibility to trade in and out of positions even while the event is ongoing. The share price dynamically represents the market's probability estimate. Additionally, liquidity providers are compensated and impermanent loss mitigated for providing AMM liquidity.</p><h4 id="">The Community Aspect</h4><p id="">The pool-based betting system on DELPHIBETS fosters a community where bettors are pitted against each other rather than against the house. This setup enhances transparency and engagement, making each wager not just an opportunity to win but also a social experience.</p><h4 id="">The Dynamic Element</h4><p id="">Unlike traditional betting platforms where odds are fixed, DELPHIBETS introduces a dynamic element where the odds can fluctuate based on the size of the pool and the number of winners. This absence of fixed odds can lead to substantial winnings, especially in events with unexpected outcomes.</p><h3 id="">Social Features</h3><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1600px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/6570924a463f16e49df2411b_R8CHKLRWCOANObHvv5a8xEMzSBmXeyp0pn7JmvjdfATUTt_Va2VefRlHQx5kXtrOJIy8v5W5WqNw6IcztC_OqwFmcnYb5J61a53eA6jzJfMg7Nl-HapDrZAbTd1cT8jtM_GZJGqXBFkPrvKeFeCleUs.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">The protocol incorporates socializing features that allow users to interact by means of a social network. This adds a layer of community engagement that goes beyond mere transactional interactions. It's about cultivating a digital space where connectivity flourishes, insights can be shared, and collective wisdom thrives.</p><h3 id="">Immediate Claim</h3><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1116px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1116px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/6570924adedbef50473332f7_lHNHBV7tENAbAggQyphLd7hd7Mufwfphgfwzgdz9B_VZnlpkJNTPU1lgiReTPwnZEgPI5Tu6Bdqk_qM01Wy3waoNl1jZ9jdN5RDVuLPMVOudhfbQcJldb0yiLLA0wEMAnTceGXgB02_WnOPHZtW54K0.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">One of the standout features is the immediate claim mechanism. Smart contracts make any winnings immediately claimable.&nbsp;</p><h2 id="">The Team: A Melting Pot of Expertise</h2><p id="">The DELPHIBETS team is a diverse group of professionals united by a common mission—to redefine the world of decentralized prediction markets.</p><p id="">Martin, the Project Manager, brings to the table a rich background in finance and entrepreneurship.</p><p id="">Jonas, responsible for Marketing,&nbsp; leverages his expertise in behavioral analysis to build a robust community and fruitful partnerships.</p><p id="">Laurent, with a remarkable track record in digital marketing, spearheads DELPHIBETS’ social media and public relations strategies.</p><p id="">Chris and Felix, the Scrypto Development experts, focus on automating complex mechanisms within the platform.</p><p id="">Marc, the UX &amp; Interface Design lead, is committed to delivering an unparalleled user experience.</p><p id="">The team is further strengthened by advisors like Florian Pieper and Marco, who offer invaluable insights into backend development and strategic decision-making and the community moderators Jonathan and Marco, both of whom have extensive knowledge about Radix and are highly committed beyond the boundaries of DELPHIBETS.</p><h2 id="">The Inception of DELPHIBETS</h2><p id="">The concept of DELPHIBETS was born within the German Radix Telegram community. What began as casual entertainment among community members soon evolved into a serious, DeFi venture. The team's collective experience and expertise have been instrumental in designing an innovative platform that addresses the limitations of existing prediction market providers.</p><h2 id="">Why Radix?</h2><blockquote id="">Radix offers a host of advantages that make it the ideal platform for DELPHIBETS.
