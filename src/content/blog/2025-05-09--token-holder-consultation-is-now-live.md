<p id="">The Radix Foundation has opened the Token Holder Consultation on the proposed repurposing of the 2.4 billion XRD Stablecoin Reserve. The consultation runs from <strong id="">12:00 UTC on May 9th until 23:59 UTC on May 19th, 2025</strong>.</p><p id="">This is your opportunity to input into the long-term direction of the Radix ecosystem.</p><p id=""><strong id="">What’s being proposed:</strong></p><ul id=""><li>Allocate <strong id="">1 billion XRD</strong> to a multi-season incentives campaign to grow on-chain activity and liquidity</li><li>Create a <strong id="">1 billion XRD Growth Fund</strong> for strategic initiatives like T1 listings, bridges, etc.</li><li><strong id="">Shorten the emission schedule by 20 years</strong>, reducing the max XRD supply by approximately 6 billion&nbsp;</li><li>Hold <strong id="">400 million XRD</strong> in reserve for future use or burn<br><br></li></ul><p id=""><strong id="">How to participate:</strong></p><p id="">Go to<a id="" href="https://consultation.radixdlt.com/"> consultation.radixdlt.com</a> and signal your support or opposition using your Radix Wallet.&nbsp;</p><p id="">No transaction is required - signaling uses <strong id="">ROLA proofs</strong>, a secure off-ledger method that’s gas-free, privacy-preserving, and verifiable.</p><p id="">Signals are weighted based on the average XRD (or equivalent) held in your account(s) during the consultation window. Foundation-owned and high-concentration entities (&gt;1B XRD) are excluded to ensure the outcome reflects the broader community.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Further details on the incentive campaign can be found </strong><a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/backing-growth-radix-incentives-campaign-proposal"><strong id="">here</strong></a><strong id="">.</strong></p><p id=""><strong id="">More details on the consultation process can be found </strong><a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/token-holder-consultation-repurposing-the-stablecoin-reserve"><strong id="">here</strong></a><strong id="">.&nbsp;</strong></p><p>‍</p>
