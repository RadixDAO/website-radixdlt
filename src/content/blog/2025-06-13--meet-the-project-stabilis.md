Inspired by Liquity V2, it has just launched.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1600px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/684ad5c0ad20a9327e58eba9_AD_4nXd_8Tclv9M0xI82cIrGn6zWelAjSlU_Smp278ePYIu3Clkv7C_Tj22Y6CrydNMczsIKY9BPNREJdhh85cOP2ED31cLVA7GEUlHB9NHhrUJhwr-gQk16DgERTF38_gTEFBw9odZEgg.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">‍</p><p id="">The promise of DeFi has always been compelling: financial systems that operate without intermediaries, governed by code rather than corporations. But the reality has often fallen short due to manual governance processes, centralized decision-making, and stability mechanisms that rely on human intervention rather than market forces.</p><p id="">Enter Stabilis, a project that’s pushing the boundaries of what's possible in decentralized finance. Built on Radix and supported by the <a id="" href="https://developers.radixdlt.com/grants">Radix Grants Program</a>, Stabilis is offering a step toward truly algorithmic governance and market-driven stability.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Beyond Traditional Stablecoins</strong></h3><p id="">At its core, Stabilis is the fusion of two components: the <strong id="">STAB Protocol</strong> and <strong id="">ILIS DAO</strong>. Together, they're pioneering what they call "fair-priced stability", a concept that challenges how we think about stable assets in crypto.</p><p id="">The STAB Protocol doesn't rely on the traditional stablecoin playbook. Instead of manually set interest rates or rigid pegging mechanisms, it uses a dynamic, market-driven algorithm that automatically adjusts borrowing and lending costs based on real-time market conditions. This means STAB maintains relative stability against the US dollar not through force, but through intelligent market responses.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The First Truly Algorithmic DAO on Radix</strong></h3><p id="">What sets Stabilis apart is its governance model. ILIS DAO operates as the first completely algorithmic DAO on Radix, where every decision is executed through smart contracts without human intervention.</p><p id="">It’s more than just a technical achievement, it's a philosophical statement about the future of decentralized organizations. While other DAOs struggle with voter apathy, governance attacks, and centralized influence, ILIS DAO eliminates these issues by removing human bias from the equation entirely.</p><p id="">The result? A transparent, trustless protocol that makes decisions based on predetermined algorithms rather than the whims of token holders or core teams.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The Mind Behind the Innovation</strong></h3><p id="">The driving force behind Stabilis is a Dutch developer known in the community as “Octopus”. With a background in Physics and a deep fascination with self-regulating systems, Octopus brings a unique perspective to DeFi that's rooted in scientific principles rather than financial engineering alone.</p><p id="">"Radix first caught my attention after the release of <a id="" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pQXaTIm1bNY">RadFi</a>," Octopus explains. "It approached DeFi in ways that addressed many of the concerns I'd long had with crypto, and it left a strong impression."</p><p id="">What started as experimentation with Scrypto, Radix's smart contract language, quickly evolved into something much more ambitious. Octopus's background in Physics naturally led to questions about complex, self-regulating systems, and those questions became the foundation for Stabilis.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">What's Next: Introducing Flux</strong></h3><p id="">While STAB continues to evolve, the ILIS DAO has already launched its next major release: <strong id="">Flux</strong>, a new stablecoin designed to maintain a precise $1 peg.</p><p id="">Flux represents the next evolution in the Stabilis ecosystem, building on the lessons learned from STAB while introducing a more traditional price-stable asset. Inspired by Liquity V2, Flux features interest rates set by users through a decentralized mechanism, creating what the team calls "a powerful and flexible financial primitive for the Radix ecosystem."</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Why This Matters for Radix</strong></h3><p id="">Stabilis demonstrates what's possible when you combine innovative technology with principled design. By choosing Radix as their foundation, the team has been able to focus on solving fundamental problems in DeFi rather than working around platform limitations.</p><p id="">The technical capabilities of Radix, combined with the support of the Radix Grants Program, have allowed Stabilis to progress from early experiments to functional prototypes that are already live and serving users.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The Bigger Picture</strong></h3><p id="">As the Radix ecosystem continues to mature, projects like Stabilis are becoming essential infrastructure by establishing new standards for what decentralized finance can achieve.</p><p id="">Whether you're a developer building the next generation of dApps or simply someone interested in the evolution of stable assets, Stabilis represents a compelling vision of DeFi's future: one where algorithms replace politics, markets determine prices, and true decentralization isn't just an aspiration, it's a reality.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Ready to explore Stabilis?</strong> Visit their<a id="" href="https://ilikeitstable.com/"> website</a>, follow their progress on<a href="https://x.com/ilisdao"> X</a>, or join the conversation in their<a href="http://t.me/ilisdao"> Telegram channel</a>.</p><p id=""><em id="">Stabilis is one of many innovative projects supported by the Radix Grants Program.
