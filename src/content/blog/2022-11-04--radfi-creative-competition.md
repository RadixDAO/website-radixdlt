<p id="">RadFi, our biggest event in Q4 2022, is coming close!</p><p id="">So with that in mind, we hope to see the joint effort of the Radix community promoting this one of a kind event!&nbsp;</p><p id="">This creative competition will be about <strong id="">promoting RadFi and encouraging others to tune in on December 8th</strong> and we are offering $5,000 of XRD as prizes for the best 10 entries.</p><ul id=""><li id="">1st, 2nd, 3rd will receive $1000 XRD each;</li><li id="">4th 5th $500 XRD each;</li><li id="">6th 7th 8th 9th 10th receive $200 XRD each.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">The competition starts on 4th November 2022&nbsp; at 13:00 UTC and ends on 30th November at 12:00 UTC. The winners will be announced on the 10th of December.&nbsp;</p><p id="">You can create anything you like, as long as it's original and helps push awareness about RadFi!&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p id="">Unsure what that means?&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p id="">Let's put it this way.&nbsp;</p><ul id=""><li id="">You could paint a 2mx2m oil canvas portrait of Babylon Betanet and RadFi.&nbsp;&nbsp;</li><li id="">You could record a “How to get to know Radix in 5 minutes? Watch RadFi live on Dec 8th” tutorial.&nbsp;</li><li id="">You can do a viral short video calling people to register their name in the RadFi landing page.&nbsp;</li><li id="">Or you can&nbsp; write a blog piece on “5 things you have to do in December” that mentions watching RadFi as one of the activities.&nbsp;</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">You could write a poem, a graphic novel, draw a meme in MS Paint, produce a TV advertisement, build a sandcastle or ice sculpture, you could even put a show on broadway. The ball is in your court on this one. Do what you are good at, what you are passionate about, and create something that highlights the biggest event of Radix this quarter: RadFi&nbsp;</p><p id="">‍</p><p id="">More about RadFi can be found <a id="" href="https://go.radixdlt.com/RadixCommunity">here.</a></p><p id="">‍</p><p id="">So get creative! Do something that stands out. Don’t worry if you are not a professional designer or artist. The judges also take into consideration the effort and love that you put into your creation.</p><p id="">‍</p><p id=""><strong id="">How to Enter:</strong></p><p id="">Once you have finished your creation, share it via Twitter using $XRD, @RadixDLT, #RadFi2022 and hashtags of top #20 projects ($ETH, $ADA$, $BNB, etc.) as well as the Discord Creative Competition channel. If it's a video, image, or poem you may need to host it on Imgur or YouTube and share the link via Twitter &amp; Discord.</p><p id="">It must be Radix or RadFi-related, not vulgar, offensive, and not touch any sensitive subjects. Must be original to be considered.</p><p id="">All entries are subject to the Radix competition <a id="" href="/assets/60a6b9a7c451ed7827760cc6_Radix-PRIZE-COMPETITIONS-TERMS-V4.0.pdf"><strong id="">terms and conditions</strong></a><strong id="">.‍ </strong>&nbsp;Winners will be required to disclose their name, DOB, country of residence and provide a Radix wallet address.</p><p id="">You may submit as many entries as you like. The RDX Works Marketing team will judge entries on originality, creativity, and community response.&nbsp;</p>
