<p id=""><strong id="">What Is Radix?</strong></p><p id="">Radix is a <strong id="">full-stack Layer-1</strong> blockchain built from the ground up for <strong id="">decentralized finance</strong>. With the <strong id="">Radix Engine,</strong> <strong id="">Scrypto smart-contract language</strong>, and <strong id="">Cerberus</strong> consensus, it offers secure asset handling, high composability between dApps, and a smooth UX designed to deliver intuitive decentralized finance at scale.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Getting Started: Install the Radix Wallet</strong></h2><ol id=""><li id="">Download the official <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/wallet"><strong id="">Radix Wallet</strong> </a>for mobile and install the <a id="" href="https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/radix-wallet-connector/bfeplaecgkoeckiidkgkmlllfbaeplgm?pli=1">Radix Wallet Connector<br></a>browser extension.</li><li id="">Complete <a id="" href="https://radquest.io"><strong id="">RadQuest</strong></a> (optional) to learn the basics of Radix transactions and UX.</li></ol><h2 id=""><strong id="">Bridging to Radix</strong></h2><p id="">To start using the Radix DeFi ecosystem, you first need to bridge assets from a supported network. You can use the Radix-native front-end for the Hyperlane permissionless bridge, <a id="" href="https://astrolescent.com/"><strong id="">Astrolescent</strong></a>.</p><p id="">The bridge allows you to send assets from other networks to Radix. You can easily transfer assets like <strong id="">USDC, wBTC, or ETH</strong> from chains like Ethereum, Arbitrum, Solana, Base, BSC, and others.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Step-by-Step Bridging Process with Astrolescent</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Select your details:</strong> <br><ul id=""><li id="">Select the token you wish to send <strong id="">from</strong> any of the supported chains (e.g., selling <strong id="">USDC</strong> from <strong id="">Ethereum</strong>).</li><li id="">Select the token you wish to receive <strong id="">on</strong> the Radix network (the wrapped version of the asset).</li><li id=""><strong id="">Amount:</strong> Enter the specific amount of tokens you are selling.</li></ul></li><li id=""><strong id="">Connect Wallets:</strong> Click the <strong id="">Connect Wallet</strong> button. <br><ul id=""><li id="">Connect the wallet of the chain you are <strong id="">bridging from</strong>. This wallet holds the funds you are bridging.</li><li id="">You will also connect your <strong id="">Radix Wallet</strong> to act as the recipient address for the tokens you are receiving on Radix.</li></ul></li><li id=""><strong id="">Initiate the Bridge:</strong> After reviewing all details, click the <strong id="">Bridge</strong> button. Confirm as necessary in the sending wallet after checking the details are correct.</li></ol><h2 id=""><strong id="">Explore Radix dApps</strong></h2><p id="">Radix has a growing ecosystem of decentralized applications including DEXs (<a id="" href="https://ociswap.com/"><strong id="">Ociswap</strong></a>, <a id="" href="https://www.caviarnine.com/"><strong id="">CaviarNine</strong></a>, <a id="" href="https://defiplaza.net/"><strong>DeFiPlaza</strong></a>), lending markets (<a id="" href="https://rootfinance.xyz/"><strong id="">Root Finance</strong></a>, <a id="" href="https://weft.finance/"><strong id="">Weft Finance</strong></a>), NFT platforms (<a id="" href="https://www.xrdegen.com/"><strong id="">XRDegen</strong></a>), games, and more. Because Radix is <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radix-engine-v2-an-asset-oriented-smart-contract-environment"><strong>asset-oriented</strong></a>, these dApps benefit from native token behavior, <a href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radixs-asset-oriented-transactions"><strong>human-readable transaction</strong></a><a href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radixs-asset-oriented-transactions"><strong>s</strong></a>, and true composability.</p><p id="">Certain decentralized applications, such as <a id="" href="https://atomix.fly.dev/"><strong id="">Atomix</strong></a> (a Peer-to-Peer trading platform) and <a id="" href="https://bullring-frontend.fly.dev/"><strong id="">Bullring</strong></a> (a live auction platform), are exclusively possible on Radix because they leverage the power of <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radix-subintents-in-action-peer-to-peer-trading-with-atomix"><strong id="">Subintents</strong></a>. This unique Radix feature enables advanced use cases like making a direct offer to buy an NFT or token from another user, delivered straight to their wallet. The recipient can instantly accept the offer, resulting in a <strong id="">pure peer-to-peer exchange</strong> with <strong id="">no slippage, or waiting time</strong>.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Visit the <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/ecosystem-directory"><strong id="">Radix Ecosystem Directory</strong></a> to find Radix dApps, or simply tap the <strong id="">dApps</strong> <strong id="">section</strong> in your Radix Wallet. <br><br></p><h1 id=""><strong id="">Radix Rewards</strong></h1><p id=""><a id="" href="https://incentives.radixdlt.com/"><strong id="">Radix Rewards</strong></a><strong id=""> offers up to 1 billion XRD</strong> to participants for trading, lending/borrowing or simply interacting with selected Radix dApps. You can participate by <a id="" href="https://dashboard.radixdlt.com/network-staking">delegating your <strong id="">XRD</strong> tokens to a chosen validator node</a>. This process helps secure the network's consensus mechanism without requiring you to run complex infrastructure yourself.&nbsp;</p><p id="">By staking your XRD, or holding <strong id="">LSUs</strong> (Liquid Stake Units), you not only earn the standard staking rewards but also qualify for a <strong id="">multiplier (up to 3x)</strong> on the <strong id="">Season Points (SP)</strong> you earn in Radix Rewards. This means that active participation in DeFi combined with staking your XRD maximizes your potential rewards.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Building on Radix</strong></h2><p id="">For developers, Radix is engineered to provide the shortest path from idea to secure, atomically composable dApps. At the core of this experience is <strong id="">Scrypto</strong>, Radix's <strong id="">asset-oriented</strong> smart contract programming language, which is based on <strong id="">Rust</strong>.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">How is Scrypto different?</strong></h3><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Asset-Oriented Programming:</strong> Unlike traditional languages where tokens (or "resources" in Radix terminology) are implemented by developers inside a smart contract, Scrypto makes them a <strong id="">native, first-class feature</strong> of the language and the <strong id="">Radix Engine</strong>. This means the platform itself provides the logic for secure asset behavior, removing the burden from the developer.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Security by Design:</strong> This approach makes entire classes of DeFi exploits, common on other chains, <strong id="">impossible</strong> by design. The platform handles the low-level security of assets, allowing developers to focus purely on their unique business logic.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Speed and Reusability:</strong> Scrypto splits the concept of a smart contract into a <a id="" href="https://docs.radixdlt.com/docs/blueprints-and-components"><strong id="">Blueprint</strong></a> (the reusable logic) and a <strong id="">Component</strong> (the live, instantiated contract). This, combined with the built-in asset handling, massively speeds up development. For example, a complex dApp like a DEX that might require hundreds of lines of Solidity can be written in a fraction of the code using Scrypto.</li><li id=""><strong id="">And so much more!</strong></li></ul><p id="">If you are a builder looking to create secure and innovative DeFi products, learning Scrypto is the first step.
