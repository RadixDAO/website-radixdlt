<p id="">TLDR:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Season 0 rewards are a <strong id="">bonus </strong>on top of Season 1.</li><li id="">Only active participation in Season 1 unlocks your bonus.</li><li id="">New users aren’t penalised, everyone competes on the same field.</li></ul><p id="">&nbsp;</p><p id="">Radix Rewards Season 1 is nearly here. But before the main event kicks off, it’s time to unlock an extra boost for those who backed Radix through the grind: Season 0.</p><p id="">We’ve debated the best way to do this. The goal was always clear; recognize the community’s commitment during the toughest times, while keeping the focus on growing Season 1, not diluting it.</p><p id="">Here’s the outcome:</p><p id=""><strong id="">Season 0 rewards won’t be a flat handout. They’ll act as a bonus.</strong></p><p id="">The more you participate in Season 1, the more of your Season 0 bonus you unlock.</p><p id="">This means the real winners are the people who back Radix now and into the future.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Why We Designed It This Way</strong></h2><p id=""><strong id="">1. Loyalty counts, but action wins</strong></p><p id="">If you held through the hardest months, you deserve recognition. But it wouldn’t be fair for those who left the community to receive the same reward as those who stayed. Season 0 makes sure loyalty matters, while ensuring that only those who step up in Season 1 get paid.</p><p id=""><strong id="">2. Keeping it fair and simple<br></strong>We tested a bunch of options: counting dApp usage, LPs, fees. Every approach created loopholes or distortions. In the end, <strong id="">XRD and XRD derivatives (LSUs, LSULP, and XRD deposited in dApps/pools tracked in Season 1)</strong> came out as the fairest measure of who was backing Radix. Not perfect, but it gets us closest to capturing real backing without overcomplicating the system.</p><p id=""><strong id="">3. Built for the system we’re shipping<br></strong>A flat airdrop sounds neat, but it would either reward dead wallets or force us to cut people off early. Neither is good. Making Season 0 a Season 1 multiplier ensures only active, engaged users benefit, and it aligns perfectly with the way the Radix Rewards system is designed.</p><p id=""><strong id="">4. Protecting new users<br></strong>Season 0 is extra. Season 1 rewards remain untouched. Everyone competes on the same playing field, while Season 0 gives long-term backers a well-earned boost.</p><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">How the Bonus Works</strong></h2><ul id=""><li id="">For each of the last 52 weeks, we’ll snapshot XRD and derivatives held or deployed (where trackable with what we’ve built for Season 1).<br><br></li><li id="">From that, we’ll calculate weekly Activity Points (AP), then convert them into Season Points (SP).<br><br></li><li id="">Add up all 52 weeks, and you get your <strong id="">Season 0 activity score</strong>.<br><br></li><li id="">That score translates into a <strong id="">Season 1 bonus</strong>, expected to range from <strong id="">+1% up to +20%</strong> on top of whatever you earn in Season 1.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">But here’s the key: <strong id="">you only get it if you actively participate in Season 1.</strong></p><p id="">Holding XRD alone does nothing. You have to show up, trade, LP, lend, stake. Passive whales get nothing. Active participants win. Season 0 isn’t about the past, it’s about kickstarting the future.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">What This Means for You</strong></h2><ul id=""><li id="">Season 0 rewards are <strong id="">a boost to your Season 1 rewards, not a handout</strong>.<br><br></li><li id="">Past commitment is recognized, but participation today is what pays.<br><br></li><li id="">No inactive wallets or double-counting.
