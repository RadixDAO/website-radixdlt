<h4 id=""><strong id="">Overview - Piers Ridyard, CEO</strong></h4><p id="">The sudden de-pegging of UST and the resultant hyperinflation of Luna continues to have ramifications for the wider crypto market. These market echos are going to keep coming - we have not yet seen the full pain that this is going to inflict, both to teams that used UST as part of their treasury (we did not, and do not speculate with our team treasury), as well as the hostile regulatory response that could occur.</p><p id="">However, all is not doom and gloom - the purpose of open source code is not to create perfect code the first time, but to iterate towards something safe, resilient and reliable. The same is true with the open source financial system that everyone in the DeFi/Web3.0 industry is currently building. The end goal of the system is the same - safe, resilient and reliable finance - one that presents an open platform for anyone to use and compete for a global audience.</p><p id="">These times are the times when we learn the most, filter for what has worked and what is probably not going to work, and then rebuild something better. The 2017 ICO boom taught us the importance of secondary market liquidity. The 2022 DeFi crash is teaching us the dangers of self-referencing systems and leverage.&nbsp;</p><p id="">The difference between a bull market and a bear market has always fascinated me. Major innovations in the crypto space only really seem to come out during bear markets. I am a huge fan of the concept of <a id="" href="https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/25744928-deep-work">Deep Work</a> - a thing that is only possible when the noise of the outside world is reduced so that slower, deeper thoughts can emerge. I have high conviction that these market cycles are a huge contributing factor to making Deep Work possible for great teams working in the crypto space.</p><p id="">Having gone through roughly three bull/bear cycles now, this period is actually exciting for me. While capital drives the markets over the short term, it is real value creation that drives the markets over the long term. As Radix has always been built around long term value creation, this feels like a massive opportunity as the next cycle is where I feel like Radix is going to be in an incredibly strong position for the next phase of DeFi and Web 3.0 - a position that has only been possible after years and years of building and testing - plus the phenomenal Scrypto and Radix community.</p><p id="">Scrypto and the Radix Engine keep exceeding expectations, and we are already seeing the first glimmers of what can truly be achieved with a usable set of tools for building DeFi. In my (biased) opinion, the next year is going to be a bumper year for the next wave of innovation for users of DeFi and Web 3.0 - all powered by Radix.</p><p id="">Super exciting time to be alive.</p><h4 id=""><strong id="">Strategy &amp; Marketing (Adam Simmons, CSO &amp; Jeremy Epstein, CMO)</strong> </h4><p id=""><em id="">“The excellence of the initiation of any movement is its timeliness.”</em></p><p id="">-Lao Tzu, Chapter 8 of the <a id="" href="https://taoism.net/tao-te-ching-online-translation/">Tao Te Ching.</a></p><p id="">For the last 2 months, we’ve been talking about the Brave takeover campaign scheduled for May 10th.
