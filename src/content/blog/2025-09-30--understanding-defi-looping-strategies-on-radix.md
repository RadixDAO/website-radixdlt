<p id=""><em id="">This content is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Always do your own research before implementing any DeFi strategies and understand the risks involved.</em></p><p id="">DeFi looping has become one of the most popular strategies for earning enhanced yields and gaining leveraged exposure to assets. As the Radix ecosystem continues to grow with robust lending platforms and DEXs, understanding how to loop effectively can help you maximize your DeFi opportunities. This tutorial will walk you through what looping is, how it works on Radix, and the risks you need to consider.</p><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">What is DeFi Looping?</strong></h2><p id="">Looping is a strategy where you deposit collateral into a lending platform, borrow against it, swap the borrowed assets for more of your original collateral, and repeat the process multiple times. Think of it as creating a leverage position through recursive borrowing and lending.</p><p id="">The strategy essentially allows you to:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Gain leveraged exposure to an asset without using derivatives</li><li id="">Earn lending rewards on a larger collateral position</li><li id="">Potentially earn borrowing rewards if the platform offers incentives (e.g., Root Points on Root Finance)</li><li id="">Increase your capital efficiency in DeFi protocols</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1600px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/68dbfe4da3c03783e4a18528_2f2d1466.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><h2 id=""><strong id="">How Does Looping Work on Radix?</strong></h2><p id="">On Radix, you can implement looping strategies using lending platforms like <a id="" href="https://rootfinance.xyz/">Root</a> or <a id="" href="https://weft.finance/">WEFT</a>. Here's the basic flow:</p><ol id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Deposit collateral</strong> (e.g., XRD) into Root or WEFT</li><li id=""><strong id="">Borrow stablecoins</strong> (e.g., hUSDT) against your collateral</li><li id=""><strong id="">Swap the borrowed stablecoins</strong> for more XRD on <a id="" href="https://ociswap.com/">Ociswap</a>, <a id="" href="https://www.caviarnine.com/">CaviarNine</a>, or <a id="" href="https://astrolescent.com/">Astrolescent</a></li><li id=""><strong id="">Deposit the new XRD</strong> back as collateral</li><li id=""><strong id="">Repeat the process</strong> until you reach your desired leverage</li></ol><p id="">Each loop increases your exposure to XRD price movements while potentially increasing your yield from lending rewards.</p><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Key Risks to Understand</strong></h2><h3 id=""><strong id="">1. Liquidation Risk</strong></h3><p id="">The biggest risk in looping is liquidation. When your health factor drops below 1.0, the lending protocol automatically liquidates your position to protect lenders.</p><p id=""><strong id="">When Liquidation is Triggered:</strong></p><ol id=""><li id="">A liquidator (bot or user) repays part or all of your debt</li><li id="">They receive your collateral at a discount</li><li id="">The protocol may charge additional fees (see protocol documentation)</li><li id="">Your position is forcibly closed automatically, you have no choice in this</li></ol><p id="">‍</p><p id=""><strong id="">Why Liquidation is a Risk:</strong></p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">You sell during a price drop</strong>: Liquidation happens during price drops, locking in your losses</li><li id=""><strong id="">You don't get full value</strong>: Liquidators take your collateral at a discount (their profit is your loss)</li><li id=""><strong id="">Double penalty</strong>: You pay both the liquidation bonus to liquidators AND a penalty fee to the protocol</li><li id=""><strong id="">No recovery option</strong>: Once liquidated, your position is closed</li><li id=""><strong id="">Cascade effect</strong>: Partial liquidations can trigger full liquidations as your leverage increases</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">2. Interest Rate Risk</strong></h3><p id="">You're paying borrow interest on hUSDT while earning supply interest on XRD. If the borrow rate exceeds your earnings, you'll have negative carry. <br><br><strong id="">What is negative carry? <br></strong>Simply put, negative carry means you are losing money by holding the position. If you are paying 8% to borrow but are only earning 5% on your collateral, you are losing 3% annually - even if the price does not move.&nbsp;</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">3. Price Risk</strong></h3><p id="">Leverage amplifies both gains and losses. With our 2.53x leveraged position:</p><ul id=""><li id="">If XRD rises 10%, you gain ~25%</li><li id="">If XRD falls 10%, you lose ~25%</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">However, on the downside, liquidation may cut your losses before they reach the full leveraged percentage. In our example, a 13-14% price drop would trigger liquidation, meaning you'd lose most of your initial collateral before experiencing the full theoretical loss. However, the cost of liquidation explained above could be even greater.</p><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Step-by-Step Example: XRD/hUSDT Loop on Root</strong></h2><p id="">Let's walk through a practical example using XRD and hUSDT on <a id="" href="https://rootfinance.xyz/">Root</a>, assuming:</p><ul id=""><li id="">You start with 10,000 XRD</li><li id="">Root's XRD collateral factor is 70% (you can borrow up to 70% of your collateral value)</li><li id="">Exchange rate: 1 hUSDT = 20 XRD (example rate)</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Loop 1: Initial Position</strong></h3><ul id=""><li id="">Deposit 10,000 XRD as collateral</li><li id="">Your collateral value in hUSDT terms: 500 hUSDT equivalent</li><li id="">Borrow 350 hUSDT (70% of 500)</li><li id="">Swap 350 hUSDT for 7,000 XRD on a DEX</li><li id="">Deposit the 7,000 XRD back into Root</li><li id=""><strong id="">New total collateral:</strong> 17,000 XRD</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Loop 2: Building Leverage</strong></h3><ul id=""><li id="">Your collateral: 17,000 XRD (850 hUSDT equivalent value)</li><li id="">Total borrowing capacity: 595 hUSDT (70% of 850)</li><li id="">Already borrowed: 350 hUSDT</li><li id="">Borrow an additional 245 hUSDT (595 - 350)</li><li id="">Swap 245 hUSDT for 4,900 XRD</li><li id="">Deposit back into Root</li><li id=""><strong id="">New total collateral:</strong> 21,900 XRD</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Loop 3: Approaching Target</strong></h3><ul id=""><li id="">Your collateral: 21,900 XRD (1,095 hUSDT equivalent value)</li><li id="">Total borrowing capacity: 766.5 hUSDT (70% of 1,095)</li><li id="">Already borrowed: 595 hUSDT</li><li id="">Borrow an additional 171.5 hUSDT (766.5 - 595)</li><li id="">Swap 171.5 hUSDT for 3,430 XRD</li><li id="">Deposit back into Root</li><li id=""><strong id="">New total collateral:</strong> 25,330 XRD</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1600px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/68dbfe4da3c03783e4a18525_d3f0ef1f.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">‍</p><p id="">After three loops, you've turned your 10,000 XRD position into 25,330 XRD of exposure (2.53x leverage), while borrowing 766.5 hUSDT total.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Important Note:</strong> At 2.53x leverage with a 70% collateral factor, your health factor is approximately 1.15. This means you have limited buffer before liquidation - a price drop could trigger liquidation. If liquidated, the protocol automatically sells your collateral to repay the debt, and you lose most of your initial investment. This illustrates why conservative leverage targets are crucial.</p><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Calculating Your Maximum Leverage</strong></h2><p id="">The maximum theoretical leverage you can achieve through looping depends on the collateral factor (loan-to-value ratio):</p><p id=""><strong id="">Maximum Leverage = 1 / (1 - Collateral Factor)</strong></p><p id="">For example:</p><ul id=""><li id="">70% collateral factor = 1 / (1 - 0.70) = 1 / 0.30 = 3.33x maximum theoretical leverage</li><li id="">80% collateral factor = 1 / (1 - 0.80) = 1 / 0.20 = 5x maximum theoretical leverage</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id=""><strong id="">Important:</strong> Never loop to the theoretical maximum! This would leave you with zero buffer and instant liquidation risk. Always maintain a safety margin. For a 70% collateral factor, target 2-2.5x leverage maximum.</p><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">The Economics of Looping</strong></h2><p id="">When you loop, you're essentially:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Earning</strong>: Supply APY on your total collateral position</li><li id=""><strong id="">Paying</strong>: Borrow APY on your total debt</li><li id=""><strong id="">Exposed to</strong>: Amplified price movements of your collateral asset</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">Your net APY = [(Supply APY × Total Collateral Value) - (Borrow APY × Total Debt Value)] / Initial Capital</p><p id="">For example, with our 2.53x leveraged position:</p><ul id=""><li id="">If XRD supply APY is 5% and hUSDT borrow APY is 8%</li><li id="">You earn: 5% on 25,330 XRD of collateral</li><li id="">You pay: 8% on 766.5 hUSDT of debt</li><li id="">Your net return depends on the rate differential and XRD price movement</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">This means looping is most profitable when:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Supply rates exceed borrow rates (positive carry)</li><li id="">You're bullish on the collateral asset</li><li id="">The rate differential outweighs the borrowing costs</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Risk Management Best Practices</strong></h2><p id=""><strong id="">Set Conservative Targets</strong>. Don't loop to maximum leverage. With a 70% collateral factor:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Maximum theoretical: 3.33x (extremely dangerous with a high risk of liquidation)</li><li id="">Our example: 2.53x (still risky with 13-14% liquidation threshold)</li><li id="">Safer: 2.0-2.2x (provides 20-25% price buffer)</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">Target 50-70% of the theoretical maximum for better safety.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Monitor Health Factor</strong>: Keep your loan health factor above 1.5 on Root or WEFT. This gives you a buffer during market volatility.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Calculate Your Liquidation Price</strong>: Before looping, know exactly at what price ratio you'll be liquidated. The formula:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Health Factor = (Collateral Value × Collateral Factor) / Borrowed Value</li><li id="">You're liquidated when Health Factor &lt; 1.0</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">For our 2.53x leverage example:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Health Factor starts at ~1.15</li><li id="">This means only a ~13-14% price drop could trigger liquidation</li><li id="">At maximum leverage (3.33x), even smaller price movements become dangerous</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">Always calculate based on your exact collateral, debt, and liquidation thresholds. The higher your leverage, the smaller the price movement needed to liquidate you.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Have an Exit Strategy</strong>: Know how to unwind your position:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Withdraw some collateral</li><li id="">Swap to hUSDT</li><li id="">Repay part of your loan</li><li id="">Repeat until fully unwound</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id=""><strong id="">Start Small</strong>: Test with a small position first to understand the mechanics and associated costs.</p><p id="">‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Alternative Looping Strategies</strong></h2><h3
