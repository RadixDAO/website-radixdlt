<p id="">Alexandria, the latest stop on the Radix roadmap is finally&nbsp;<a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/alexandria-scrypto-is-here">here</a>. </p><p id="">Scrypto, the game-changer for DeFi development, <a id="" href="https://developers.radixdlt.com/?launch">is ready to go</a>. Scrypto is a breath of fresh air in DeFi development, allowing devs to be creative and push the envelope of what is possible. </p><p id=""><strong id="">So with that in mind, we want to see how creative the Radix community can be.</strong></p><p id="">This is a Radix/Scrypto themed creative competition, and we are offering up $5,000 of XRD as prizes.&nbsp;<br></p><ul id=""><li id="">1st, 2nd, 3rd get $1000 XRD each</li><li id="">4th 5th $500 XRD each</li><li id="">6th 7th 8th 9th 10th get $200 XRD each</li></ul><p id="">‍<br>You can create <strong id=""><em id="">anything</em></strong> you like, as long as it's original and related to Radix and Scrypto! </p><p id="">Unsure what that means?&nbsp;&nbsp;<br></p><p id="">Let's put it this way. You could paint a 2mx2m oil canvas portrait of our great and powerful leader Dan Hughes. You could record a “How to Build in Scrypto” tutorial or write a blog piece on your experiences with Solidity vs Scrypto. </p><p id="">You could write a poem, a graphic novel, draw a meme in MS Paint, produce a TV advertisement, build a sandcastle or ice sculpture, you could even put a show on broadway.&nbsp;The ball is in your court on this one. Do what you are good at, what you are passionate about, and create something that highlights the unfair advantage of Scrypto or your favorite aspect of Radix.<br></p><p id="">So get creating! Do something that stands out. Don’t worry if you are not a professional designer or artist. Must be original to be considered.</li><li id="">All entries are subject to the Radix competition<a id="" href="/assets/60a6b9a7c451ed7827760cc6_Radix-PRIZE-COMPETITIONS-TERMS-V4.0.pdf"> terms and conditions.</a>‍</li><li id="">You may submit as many entries as you like. </li><li id="">Judges decisions are final, we will not debate results. </li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id=""><strong id="">Details of the Competition</strong>&nbsp;</p><ul id=""><li id="">$5000 of XRD will be shared between 10 winners. 1st, 2nd, 3rd get $1000 XRD each. 4th 5th $500 XRD each, 6th 7th 8th 9th 10th get $200 XRD each.
