<blockquote id=""><strong id="">TL;DR:</strong> The 1 billion XRD Radix Rewards campaign is ready. But should we launch now, or wait ~4–6 weeks for Hyperlane and a better onboarding experience? We believe waiting could significantly amplify the campaign's impact, but we want your input before making a final call.</blockquote><p id="">Over the past few months, the Radix community has watched the Radix Rewards incentives campaign take shape, from proposal to last week's public test. The Public Test was a major step forward. It proved that the backend logic, activity tracking, and XRD multiplier system all function as expected. In short, the system's foundations are ready.</p><p id="">Here’s what was tested:</p><ul id=""><li id="">User activity across 5 dApps (CaviarNine, Ociswap, DeFiPlaza, Root Finance, and WEFT Finance).</li><li id="">Usage of bridged assets (xUSDC, xwBTC, xETH, xUSDT), and we're ready to include 7 native Radix altcoins, although this wasn't tested.</li><li id="">Fees paid, components interacted with, and real user behaviour.</li><li id="">XRD multiplier logic (balances of XRD, LSU, LSULP, and dApp deposits).</li></ul><p id="">The system held up, and feedback has helped us refine both the logic and user experience. The front-end is now being polished, and final tweaks are underway. We could launch within 2 weeks.</p><p id="">There is a strategic question we would like to ask the community first.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Should we wait until Hyperlane is live to launch the campaign?</strong></p><p id="">There’s already been community debate across Telegram and in DMs, with strong arguments both for launching now and for waiting. A poll was also created in a community channel, attracting over 170 votes. The result was close, 56% in favour v’s 44% against.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p id="">Given the importance of the incentives campaign in our long-term strategy, and that the topic was raised by the community, we’re bringing the question to you formally rather than making the decision unilaterally.</p><p id="">Having read the initial discussions and been involved in them since, here’s why we think it might be worth the short delay:</p><ol id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Better onboarding = better outcomes.</strong> With Hyperlane, users can bring assets from 100+ chains directly into Radix with less friction. That means a smoother onboarding experience, fewer drop-offs, and a broader user base from day one.</li><li id=""><strong id="">The wait is short, and well defined.</strong> Hyperlane is on track for code completion by mid-August. Audits are scheduled. Mainnet deployment is likely in late August.</li><li id=""><strong id="">The ecosystem will shift.</strong> Once Hyperlane goes live, new wrapped versions of tokens like USDC and ETH will become the default. Launching before that risks confusion or disruption mid-season.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Avoiding mid-season pivots.</strong> Switching incentivized assets or flows partway through a campaign is a headache for users, dApps, and the rewards system. Waiting avoids unnecessary friction.</li><li id=""><strong id="">We can launch with more.</strong> A few extra weeks allow us an opportunity to support more assets, refine the front-end, and polish the campaign UX based on test feedback.</li><li id=""><strong id="">A stronger first impression.</strong> Launching alongside Hyperlane and the Hyperscale testnet sends a powerful signal about the Radix ecosystem’s momentum.</li></ol><p id="">We’re not suggesting perfection over progress. But we’ve also learnt, painfully in the past, that launches matter. A great campaign experience will help drive sustained usage, capital, and ecosystem growth.</p><p id="">That said, we know there are good arguments for launching sooner. Some in the community may prefer momentum now over strategy later. That’s why we’re putting it to you.</p><p id=""><strong id="">As XRD holders, your opinion shapes how we proceed.</strong></p><p id=""><strong id="">Please take a moment to share your view in the token holder consultation:</strong><a id="" href="https://consultation.radixdlt.com"> https://consultation.radixdlt.com</a></p><p id="">You can choose:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Launch ASAP</strong> with the current MVP setup.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Wait for Hyperlane</strong> and launch with broader reach and less friction.</li></ul><p id="">Thanks for helping shape what comes next.</p>
