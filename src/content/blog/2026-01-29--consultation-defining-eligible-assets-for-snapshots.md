<p id="">As we transition to the community-led model, we must define exactly <em id="">who</em> has a voice in consultations, and how that is measured. This consultation has also been raised in the community as a way to indicate membership eligibility for a potential future community governance process.</p><p id="">Historically, governance votes often count only tokens held in accounts. However, Radix is a DeFi protocol. A significant amount of XRD is actively used in the ecosystem from staking, to providing liquidity in DEXs, in lending in markets like Weft or Root, or locked in time-release contracts.</p><p id="">Currently, we take all these XRD into account when doing consultations as we believe you should not have to withdraw liquidity or stop using DeFi applications just to participate in a consultation.</p><p id="">However, we have seen discussions about the benefits to simplify this or ensure only long term holding (like staking) is counted. Therefore, we are running a consultation to confirm which assets should be included in future governance snapshots.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The Question</strong></h3><p id=""><strong id="">What assets should be included in consultation snapshots?</strong></p><p id="">We are asking the community to define the "Safe List" of assets that represent valid voting power.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The Options</strong></h3><p id="">You may select any and <strong id="">all </strong>options that you believe should count towards a user's XRD voting weight.</p><p id=""><strong id="">1. XRD Staked on a Top 100 Validator</strong>: Tokens actively securing the network via the top 100 consensus validators.</p><p id=""><strong id="">2. XRD Staked on ANY Validator:</strong> Tokens staked to validators outside the top 100. These tokens do not earn rewards, but they signal intent to support specific node runners.</p><p id=""><strong id="">3. Liquid XRD held in an account:</strong> XRD sitting in your wallet, not staked and not engaged in DeFi.</p><p id=""><strong id="">4. XRD or LSUs in Supported DEX Pools*:</strong> Capital that is providing liquidity. <em id="">Example: If you deposit XRD and hUSDC into a pool, the XRD portion of that position counts towards your vote.</em></p><p id=""><strong id="">5. Lent XRD/LSUs*:</strong> Capital supplied to lending markets (e.g., Weft, Root).&nbsp;</p><p id=""><strong id="">6. XRD/LSUs in Time-Lock Contracts*:</strong> Tokens locked in smart contracts, such as RadLock, for a fixed period.</p><p id="">*note for options 4,5 and 6, only monitored pools are calculated, as there is no default way to capture all positions in all pools/dapps.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Consultation Process</strong></h3><p id="">This is not a "winner takes all" vote, and we’re trying to understand which of these has broad community support.</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Format:</strong> Multiple Choice (Select all that apply).</li><li id=""><strong id="">Threshold:</strong> Any individual option that receives <strong id="">&gt;60% acceptance</strong> from participants will be adopted as the standard for future consultations.</li></ul><p id="">If an option receives less than 60% support, it will be excluded from future snapshots until further notice.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Timeline</strong></h3><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Start Date:</strong> Now</li><li id=""><strong id="">End Date:</strong> Wednesday, Feb 4th, 2026 (23:59 UTC)</li></ul><p id="">Governance requires a clear definition of membership. If we only staked XRD in accounts, we ignore the most active DeFi users. If we count everything without limits, we risk gamification.</p><p id="">This consultation allows you to input on what should be considered in vote weight. The consultation is live now here:&nbsp;<a href="https://consultation.radixdlt.com">https://consultation.radixdlt.com/</a></p>
