<h2 id="">Overview </h2><p id=""><em id="">Piers Ridyard, CEO - Radix DLT</em></p><p id="">Never a dull moment in crypto as NFTs (non-fungible tokens) have their break-through moment and the foremost NFT artist, <a id="" href="https://twitter.com/beeple">Beeple</a>, is thrust into the mainstream spotlight with the sale of his largest pieces to date going for $69m (of course it did) at Christies auction.</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-center" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="center"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/6087c8c3df0d37169d8119b0_CH-Beeples-NFT.jpeg" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id=""><em id="">Beeple, Everydays – The First 5000 Days NFT, 21,069 pixels x 21,069 pixels (316,939,910 bytes). Image courtesy the artist and Christie's.</em></p><p id="">While that figure is incredible on it’s own, it is being dwarfed by the volume of secondary sales on the Top Shots NFT platform that has been built by Dapper Labs on the Flow blockchain, with an incredible $322m of NFTs sold to date; dwarfing everything else in the Ethereum ecosystem.</p><p id="">NFTs have always been an interesting, hard-to-categorise function for blockchains - at Radix we see the next generation of these NFTs moving on to an even more interesting sub category of properties: <a id="" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haecceity">haecceity</a>*, or less pretentiously “thisness”</p><p id=""><em id="">Oh THIS Beeple? This was a Beeple previously owned by Elon Musk; look you can see he owned it between 2021 and 2025. </em></p><p id="">As humans, we love narrative, we love story. So we already do this a lot. Sometimes we refer to this as “sentimental value”. When we get to the stage that we understand how not only to create uniqueness in number, but turn the uniqueness of ownership history or significance into a property of the object as well that is surfaced to the user, the NFT revolution will start getting seriously crazy.</p><p id="">Good thing we are making it easy to build NFTs on Radix right?! That’s a story for another day though.
