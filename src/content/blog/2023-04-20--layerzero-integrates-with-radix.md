<p id=""><a id="" href="https://layerzero.network/">LayerZero</a> will be integrating with the Radix Public Network after the Babylon upgrade, marking an exciting new era for DeFi on Radix.&nbsp;</p><p id="">LayerZero is a blockchain messaging protocol at the forefront of innovation. Their advanced messaging infrastructure connects over 30 blockchains seamlessly, providing a transparent and secure cross-chain messaging system accessible from one user-friendly interface. Developers can create interoperable, omnichain decentralized applications (dApps) on LayerZero, which will ultimately pave the way for a unified digital asset ecosystem.</p><p id="">This collaboration will allow for seamless cross-chain communication into and out of the Radix ecosystem, enhancing the user experience and empowering developers to create more consumer-friendly decentralized applications.</p><p id="">Radix Tokens Jersey Director Piers Ridyard said <em id="">"Our mission is to make crypto accessible to everyday users. With the Radix platform being integrated with LayerZero, it is taking a significant step forward in achieving that goal. This integration will not only enhance the user experience on the Radix public ledger but also showcase the true potential of cross-chain interoperability."</em></p><p id="">By integrating LayerZero into the Radix ecosystem, Radix users and developers will have access to a broader range of services and assets, making it easier for dApps and assets to unlock omnichain functionality.</p><p id="">The integration with LayerZero will further enhance Radix's focus on providing developer-friendly tooling and network functionality that allows for the creation of exceptional consumer-friendly DeFi, helping to drive mainstream adoption of Web3. It will also allow LayerZero to showcase its powerful interoperability features to a rapidly growing ecosystem that prioritizes user experience.</p><p id=""><em id="">"We are thrilled to be working with Radix to bring our LayerZero technology to their thriving ecosystem," said LayerZero CEO and Co-Founder, Bryan Pellegrino. "This partnership aligns perfectly with our vision of creating one unified Web3 ecosystem that connects each blockchain’s developers and user community."</em></p><p id="">Both LayerZero and Radix are committed to creating a unified Web3 ecosystem that connects developers and user communities across different blockchains. With this exciting new partnership, both Radix and LayerZero are one step closer to achieving that vision.</p><p id="">To learn more about how cross chain communication and developer experience overlap for the future of public ledgers, we highly recommend <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/the-winning-duo-for-web3">this blog post</a>.</p><p id="">And lastly, you can listen to the recent DeFi Download Podcast between Piers and Bryan to gain a deeper understanding of what LayerZero is and what makes it so much more than just an “asset bridge” for public ledgers!</p><p id=""><strong id="">Website:</strong> <a id="" href="https://layerzero.network/">https://layerzero.network/<br></a><strong id="">Twitter:</strong> <a id="" href="https://twitter.com/LayerZero_Labs">https://twitter.com/LayerZero_Labs<br></a><strong id="">LayerZero Documentation:</strong> <a id="" href="https://layerzero.network/developers">https://layerzero.network/developers</a>&nbsp;</p><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-video w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="padding-bottom:56.206088992974244%" data-rt-type="video" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="" data-rt-max-height="56.206088992974244%" data-rt-dimensions="854:480" data-page-url="INBED https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2P5XHAxrVCE"><div id=""><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/2P5XHAxrVCE" scrolling="no" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="true"></iframe></div></figure>
