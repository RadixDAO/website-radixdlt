<p id="">Everyone loves a proper cup of Joe, cuppa tea, or something in between. What if you could enjoy your favorite hot beverage from a Radix mug? Well, soon you can!</p><p id="">The <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/battle-of-the-radix-swag-round-1-the-shirt">first Radix Swag contest</a> was a resounding success, with over 100 design submissions, the winning entry went live on the new <a id="" href="https://swag.radixdlt.com/">Radix Store</a>, and sold out in <strong id="">5 minutes</strong>.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Now we call you artists, designers, and creators of the Radix community to showcase your imagination and talent once again for a chance to win!</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The second contest: Design a Mug&nbsp;</strong></h3><p id="">Details of the competition are below:&nbsp;</p><ul id=""><li id="">The contest will open on 6th July</li><li id="">Submissions close on 20th July at 22:59 UTC</li><li id="">The winning entry will have their designed product featured in the Radix store, 3850 eXRD* and receive a Radix swag package</li><li id="">Two runner-ups will also be selected and will receive 1925 eXRD* each and receive a Radix swag package</li><li id="">Winners will be selected on 26th July at 22:59 UTC</li><li id="">You can make a maximum of 3 submissions</li><li id="">All entries are subject to competition <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/competition-terms">terms and conditions</a></li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">How to enter:</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id="">Create a mug design in PNG or JPEG format with at least 150 DPI (300 DPI suggested).
