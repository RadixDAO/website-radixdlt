<p id="">With <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/multi-factor-smart-accounts-arrive-on-stokenet-phase-1-is-live"><strong id="">MFA Phase 1</strong></a>, users were able to set up <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/introducing-multi-factor-smart-accounts-a-step-by-step-rollout-on-stokenet">Multi-Factor Smart Accounts</a> for the first time on Stokenet and use <strong id="">Regular Access </strong>factors to sign transactions.</p><p id="">However, once you configured your security structure, you couldn’t change it, even if you wanted to replace a factor or if you lost access to one.</p><p id=""><strong id="">MFA Phase 2</strong> solves this.</p><p id="">You can now <em id="">safely update</em> your security structure anytime by using the recovery process with Phase 2 of MFA testing on Stokenet.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">How Updating Your Security Structure Works</strong></h2><p id="">To change your security configuration, you need to present factors from <strong id="">any 2 of the 3 roles</strong>:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Regular Access</strong></li><li id=""><strong id="">Recovery Access</strong></li><li id=""><strong id="">Confirmation Access</strong></li></ul><p id="">One role starts the update, and another role confirms it.</p><p id="">You can start the update using either <strong id="">Regular</strong> or <strong id="">Recovery</strong> access, and you can confirm it with <em id="">any other role</em>.</p><p id="">This gives flexibility while still keeping your account secure.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Timed Recovery: A Safety Net</strong></h3><p id="">The <strong id="">Recovery role</strong> has one additional power: If you’ve lost access to the other roles, it can start a <strong id="">timed recovery</strong>.</p><p id="">During timed recovery, no second role is required immediately. Instead, after a predefined delay (set when you configured your account), <em id="">any role</em> can confirm the recovery and update your security structure.</p><p id="">This ensures that even if multiple devices or factors are lost, you’re never locked out permanently.</p><p id="">More in depth details can be found <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/how-radix-multi-factor-smart-accounts-work-and-what-they-can-do#multifactor-recovery-including-social-recovery">here</a>.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">How It Works in the Wallet</strong></h2><p id="">When you update your security configuration for an account or persona:</p><ol id=""><li id="">The Radix Wallet will show you a transaction to sign.</li><li id="">You’ll be asked to provide the signatures required by your current security structure.</li><li id="">If you’ve lost a factor, the Wallet will automatically guide you through skipping it and using the appropriate recovery flow.</li></ol><p id="">If you sign only with your <strong id="">Recovery role</strong>, the Wallet will show a screen explaining that you’re about to start a <strong id="">timed recovery</strong>, and what that means.</p><p id="">Once the transaction is submitted:</p><ul id=""><li id="">If you provided two roles → <strong id="">The update applies immediately.</strong></li><li id="">If only Recovery was used → <strong id="">The account enters timed recovery.</strong></li></ul><p>‍</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Understanding Timed Recovery</strong></h2><p id="">While an account is in timed recovery:</p><ul id=""><li id="">You’ll see a clear banner indicating the recovery state.</li><li id="">Tapping it will show a screen with: <br><ul id=""><li id="">The recovery details</li><li id="">The time remaining</li><li id="">Options to <strong id="">stop</strong> the recovery</li><li id="">Or <strong id="">confirm</strong> it once the countdown has finished</li></ul></li></ul><p id="">After the recovery time has fully elapsed, you can sign a transaction to confirm the new security structure. Once confirmed, your updated factors become active, and you can use them normally.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">If you regain your lost factors…</strong></h3><p id="">You can simply <strong id="">stop the timed recovery</strong> and make any changes using the normal (immediate) update process.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Updating your Security Shield in Radix Wallet</strong></h2><p id="">Before you start:</p><p id="">Make sure your wallet is connected to Stokenet (the Radix public test network) and ensure you have already setup a Security Shield on your account or persona.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Step 1. Open the security shield for your Account or Persona</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id="">Open Account Settings.</li><li id="">Tap on Security Shield.</li></ol><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-center" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="center"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/693adc3c3a57ddee7cc39863_39d4e81e.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><h3 id=""><strong id="">Step 2. Start Editing factors</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id="">Tap on <strong id="">Edit Factors</strong> button</li><li id="">Proceed with editing the factors and confirm the new structure</li></ol><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-center" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="center"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/693adc3c3a57ddee7cc3986c_2685f50b.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Step 3.1. Review and sign the transaction</strong></h3><p id="">‍</p><ol id=""><li id="">Review and sign the transaction. The Wallet will ask you for the necessary factor to be used for signing.</li></ol><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-center" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="center"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/693adc3c3a57ddee7cc3986f_af795280.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p>‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Step 3.2. Accept an Emergency Fallback if needed</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id="">If you do present only Start Recovery factors, you will be presented with the option to use Emergency Fallback, starting a timed recovery.	</li></ol><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-center" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="center"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/693adc3c3a57ddee7cc39866_a5dd3e91.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p>‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Step 4.1. Emergency Fallback status</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id="">After a timed recovery is started the appropriate status will be displayed</li></ol><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-center" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="center"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/693adc3c3a57ddee7cc39869_b0f38637.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Step 4.2. Managing Emergency Fallback</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id="">Tapping the Recovery status button, will reveal the Account recovery screen, where you will get more information about the recovery, as well the ability to Cancel or Confirm it.&nbsp;</li></ol><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-center" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="center"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/693adc3c3a57ddee7cc39872_abd187c4.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">‍</p>
