<p id="">Yield derivatives are one of the most important products in finance today. Just one kind alone, Interest Rate Derivatives (IRD), has a notional value <a id="" href="https://www.investopedia.com/ask/answers/052715/how-big-derivatives-market.asp">estimated at $488 trillion</a> of which <a id="" href="https://www.bis.org/statistics/rpfx22_ir.htm">$5 trillion is traded daily</a>. This is roughly equivalent to the <a id="" href="https://www.statista.com/statistics/421060/global-financial-institutions-assets">total assets of financial institutions worldwide</a>.</p><p id="">Signs are pointing to yield derivatives in DeFi being the next big thing:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><a id="" href="http://defillama.com">DeFi TVL</a> has doubled in the last six months while cryptoassets such as Bitcoin are seeing unprecedented institutional adoption. These assets will seek on-chain sources of yield, and that yield will create demand for yield derivatives.</li><li id="">On-chain yield-bearing assets, from <a id="" href="https://gmx.io">GLP</a> to synthetic dollars like <a id="" href="https://www.ethena.fi/">Ethena</a> to tokenized treasury bills, will continue to see rapid expansion as the only source of global yield accessible to anyone with an internet connection.</li><li id="">Yield derivative dApps have seen breakneck growth, with Pendle alone growing its total value locked (TVL) by 10x since the start of the year to more than $2 billion today.&nbsp;<br>‍</li></ul><p id="">With so much potential, there is a real opportunity for the entrepreneurs who can take the proven model from traditional finance, replicate it in DeFi, and leverage DeFi’s <a id="" href="/assets/63160cfdcf6f3b44a1527511_Understanding-Web3-The-Five-Use-Cases-that-will-Dominate-the-Next-Market-Cycle.pdf">inherent advantages (pg. 4-5)</a> to outcompete and one day exceed the original.</p><p id="">As of right now, Radix offers entrepreneurs a $250m untapped opportunity to build the yield derivatives infrastructure for the current Radix ecosystem. But for those looking ahead to tomorrow, Radix offers entrepreneurs the only layer 1 infrastructure that has a safe enough user experience (and more) to go after the remaining $488 trillion.&nbsp;</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">What is a Yield Derivative?</strong></h2><p id="">A yield derivative is a financial contract based on the interest, coupon, or dividend payment from a loan, bond, stock, or other yield-generating asset. Yield derivatives allow future cashflows to be bought or sold without needing to own the underlying asset, allowing for the tailoring - and trading - of specific kinds of financial exposures. Common examples of yield derivatives include interest rate swaps, futures, and options.</p><p id="">With yield derivatives:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Entities can hedge their risks and better control their future cashflows. For instance, a real estate company can hedge the risk of its mortgage payments going up if the central bank increases the base interest rate.</li><li id="">Funds can more predictably manage their portfolios, e.g. a pension fund that needs to ensure that an investment returns at least a certain yield above or below some base rate.</li><li id="">Speculators can bet on interest rate changes or future dividend payments and/or gain access to leverage.</li><li id="">Companies can increase their earnings from unused assets, like cash in a bank, by entering into interest rate swaps. In these deals, they trade their interest earnings for other types of payments that better suit their needs over set periods.<br>‍</li></ul><p id="">In essence, yield derivatives allow for the creation of markets between those who need predictable cashflows, those that prefer variable or leveraged cashflows, or those that don’t need cashflows in future, but need cash now.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Yield Derivatives in DeFi Today</strong></h3><p id="">In DeFi today, yield comes primarily from five sources:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Fees</strong>, such as traders paying a fee to Uniswap Liquidity Providers (LPs).</li><li id=""><strong id="">Interest</strong>, such as borrowers paying lenders an interest payment on Aave or a funding rate on GMX.&nbsp;&nbsp;</li><li id=""><strong id="">Newly minted tokens</strong>, such as a decentralized network’s Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS) system minting new emissions as a reward for securing the network.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Asset price increases</strong> that can allow financial dApps and products to pay yield.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Real World Assets (RWA) </strong>that bring external yield on-chain,<strong id=""> </strong>such as tokenized Treasury Bills that pay their holder the US federal funds rate.<br>‍</li></ul><p id="">With so many sources of on-chain yield, a new crop of dApps are stepping in to offer yield-related derivatives products:</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Pendle</strong></h3><p id="">With <a id="" href="https://defillama.com/protocol/pendle">$2.4 billion in TVL</a>, <a id="" href="http://pendle.finance">Pendle.Finance</a> is arguably the largest yield derivatives product in DeFi today. Pendle allows for an underlying yield-bearing token (such as a stETH) to be wrapped into two tokens: a Principle Token (<strong id="">PT</strong>), and a Yield Token (<strong id="">YT</strong>). Pendle calls this process <em id="">yield-tokenization</em>.</p><p id="">Each PT and YT has a maturity date:</p><ul id=""><li id="">For the PT, holders don’t receive any yield before the maturity date, but they can redeem the full underlying yield-bearing token after the maturity date, and thus have access to the full underlying token, plus any future yields, after maturity. E.g. If you own 1 PT-stETH with 1 year maturity, you will be able to redeem 1 ETH worth of stETH after 1 year.</li><li id="">For the YT, the yield from the underlying yield-bearing token is accrued up until the maturity date, after which the YT has no further value. E.g. If you own 1 YT-stETH and stETH has an average yield of 5% through the year, you will have accrued 0.05 stETH by the end of the year.</li></ul><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:808px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="808px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="__wf_reserved_inherit" src="/assets/6602d365c762c65df94fae19_Image-26-03-2024-at-13.53.jpeg" width="auto" height="auto" loading="lazy"></div></figure><p id="">The PT and YT tokens are liquid and can be traded on the Pendle AMM.&nbsp;</p><p id="">By separating principle and yield, Pendle allows users to create assets that can provide:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Fixed yield (e.g. fixed yield on stETH)</li><li id="">Long yield (e.g. bet on stETH yield going up by purchasing more yield)</li><li id="">Earn more yield without additional risks (e.g. provide liquidity with your stETH)<br>‍</li></ul><p id="">Pendle has even sparked the creation of its own ecosystem, with projects such as <a id="" href="https://docs.penpiexyz.io/">Penpie</a> or <a id="" href="https://equilibria.fi/home">Equilibria</a> building products on top of it.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Others</strong></h3><p id="">Other yield derivative dApps include <a id="" href="http://notional.finance">Notional.Finance</a>, which allows users to earn fixed, variable, or leveraged interest, by splitting the underlying yield-bearing token into fCash tokens (the principal) and cTokens (the yield token).&nbsp;</p><p id=""><a id="" href="http://timelessfi.com">Timeless</a> has a different twist - its yield tokens never expire. Timeless achieves this with a Perpetual Yield Token (PYT), whose holders receive the yield generated by user deposits, and a Negative Yield Token (NYT), whose market price moves in the opposite direction as the PYT.&nbsp;</p><p id=""><a id="" href="http://spectra.finance">Spectra.finance</a> focuses on not only supporting fixed and variable rates, but upfront yield, borrowing and lending with principal tokens as collateral, and a yield marketplace.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">The Opportunity on Radix</strong></h2><p id="">Today, 3.75 billion XRD is staked, yielding 300m XRD per year, or around 8% APY.
