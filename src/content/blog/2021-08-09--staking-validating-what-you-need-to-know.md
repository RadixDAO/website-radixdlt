<p id="">With the successful launch of <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-is-being-delivered-for-the-olympia-mainnet"><strong id="">Olympia</strong></a>, <a id="" href="https://www.instapass.io/"><strong id="">Instapass</strong></a>, and <a id="" href="http://www.instabridge.io"><strong id="">Instabridge</strong></a>, attention now shifts to staking and validating.</p><p id="">Staking is an important activity for Radix token holders, not only to earn staking rewards, but also to support the security and decentralization of the <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-are-the-radix-public-network-and-radix-ledger"><strong id="">Radix Public Network</strong></a>. To help ensure the community is prepared, we have put together the key things you need to know and do over the coming days and weeks to be ready to participate in staking on Radix.</p><p id="">Remember, there is no rush to stake immediately as there is no advantage to staking prior to the bootstrapping epoch ending (approximately Wednesday August 11), when normal 30 - 90 minute <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/whats-an-epoch"><strong id="">epochs</strong></a> commence and the <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-is-the-radix-protocol"><strong id="">Radix Protocol</strong></a> automatically enables emissions rewards. So take your time. Make the best decision. Your rewards and the Radix network are counting on you.&nbsp;</p><p id="">You can read more on the exact timeline for this initial bootstrapping period here: <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/radix-mainnet-launch-timeline"><strong id="">Radix Mainnet Launch Timeline</strong></a>.</p><p id="">If you want the TL;DR on choosing validator nodes to stake to, follow the 5-by-5 rule: Spread your stake across 5 good validators, none with more than 5% of the total stake.</p><p id="">To understand more about why this matters, read on.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">What is staking and validating, and why are <em id="">you</em> important?</strong></h3><p id="">Radix uses a <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-kind-of-staking-system-does-radix-use"><strong id="">Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)</strong></a> system to weight the votes of <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-is-a-validator-node"><strong id="">validators</strong></a> to protect the network against <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-is-a-sybil-attack"><strong id="">Sybil</strong></a> attacks.</p><p id="">This means you, the decentralized community of XRD token holders, are the ones who decide which validators verify transactions and secure the network. In the Olympia release of the Radix Public Network, the <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-is-the-radix-protocol"><strong id="">Radix Protocol</strong></a> selects the top 100 validators with the most stake at the beginning of every epoch to be in the <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-is-a-validator-set"><strong id="">validator set</strong></a> for that epoch.</p><p id="">The protocol only provides emissions XRD tokens to stakers who have delegated to validators that are in that 100 – and are correctly participating in consensus with high uptime (uptimes below 98% mean no emissions rewards for that validator).</p><p id="">Picking which validators to stake to is therefore of utmost importance – both to network operation, and to your own rewards. Before choosing who to stake to, you should thoroughly research which validator node-runners are trustworthy and have the technical and operational capability to be performant.</p><p id="">You can start with the <a id="" href="https://explorer.radixdlt.com/#/validators"><strong id="">Radix Explorer</strong></a>, which shows how much stake each validator already has delegated to it, how much stake the node’s owner has staked to their own node, the fee % the node charges, and the node’s uptime over the past 2 weeks. It also links to the website of each validator that they have provided to tell you more about themselves. You can also engage with validators through the <a id="" href="https://t.me/radix_dlt"><strong id="">Radix DLT Official Telegram</strong></a>, #node-runners channel on <a id="" href="https://discord.com/invite/WkB2USt"><strong id="">Discord,</strong> </a>the hashtag #RadixValidators on <a id="" href="https://twitter.com/search?q=%23radixvalidators"><strong id="">Twitter</strong></a>, and the <a id="" href="https://www.reddit.com/r/RadixValidators/"><strong id="">Radix Validators Subreddit</strong></a>.</p><p id="">Here are some recommendations to keep in mind when choosing the validators that you stake to:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Stake to quality</strong>. Make a judgment on the quality and trustworthiness of validator node-runners through their interactions with the community. While it may be tempting to pick validators with the lowest fee, please do also consider that running a performant validator node takes effort, skill and carries costs. Your staking rewards are only valuable if Radix is decentralized and secure, so please bear this in mind.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Distribute</strong> <strong id="">your stake</strong> across multiple validators to minimize your risk in case a validator goes offline or misses consensus <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-are-rounds-and-what-does-rounds-per-second-mean"><strong id="">rounds</strong></a>, which may result in you missing out on all of your staking rewards, as well as to encourage greater network diversity. We recommend staking to at least 5 validators that you trust.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Don't stake to the very top validators</strong>. If a validator has more than <strong id="">5%</strong> of stake (visible on <a id="" href="https://explorer.radixdlt.com/#/validators"><strong id="">Radix Explorer</strong></a>), we recommend not further increasing that stake and instead picking validators with less stake. This is important to ensure that no one validator or group of validators becomes a single point of failure.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Stake across a range of geographies and deployments</strong>. A diverse network is a more resilient network that can withstand outages at a specific physical location, or at a specific service provider (e.g. AWS).&nbsp;</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">Here are some hand-picked articles from <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/"><strong id="">Radix Learn</strong></a> to help you better understand staking and choose which validators to stake to:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/how-should-i-choose-validators-to-stake-to"><strong id="">How should I choose validators to stake to?</strong></a></li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/where-is-the-best-place-for-validators-and-the-wider-radix-community-to-connect"><strong id="">Where is the best place for validators and the wider Radix community to connect?</strong></a></li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/how-to-stake-and-unstake-xrd-in-the-desktop-wallet"><strong id="">How to Stake and Unstake XRD in the Desktop Wallet</strong></a></li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/how-xrd-staking-emissions-rewards-are-calculated-for-token-holders"><strong id="">How XRD staking emissions rewards are calculated - for token holders</strong></a><strong id="">.</strong></li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Staking information for validators</strong></h3><p id="">If you think you may have interest in running a validator node, the best technical resource for all things validating begins with <a id="" href="https://docs.radixdlt.com/main/node-and-gateway/node-introduction.html"><strong id="">An Introduction to Radix Nodes</strong></a> on the Radix Tech Docs. This should provide you all the technical details to get you going on running a validator node. But remember, running a validator node takes some technical know-how. It’s also worth reading specifically about <a id="" href="https://docs.radixdlt.com/main/node-and-gateway/validators-and-incentives.html"><strong id="">Validators and Incentives</strong></a> before deciding if node-running is for you.</p><p id="">For those that do have the technical and operational capability, we expect validating on Radix to be competitive. This means you need to actively engage with the Radix community and convince XRD token holders why they should stake with you.</p><p id="">If you are planning to be a validator, we encourage you to market yourselves to the Radix community. Explain why you have the technical know-how, how you are able and committed to operating a node reliably 24/7, and what other value you can bring to the community.</p><p id="">The channels available for this are:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><a id="" href="https://t.me/radix_dlt"><strong id="">Radix DLT Official Telegram</strong></a></li><li id="">#node-runners on <a id="" href="https://discord.com/invite/WkB2USt"><strong id="">Discord</strong></a></li><li id="">tagging <a id="" href="https://twitter.com/radixdlt"><strong id="">@radixdlt</strong></a> on Twitter and using the hashtag #RadixValidators</li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://www.reddit.com/r/RadixValidators/"><strong id="">Radix Validators Subreddit</strong></a>.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">We would appreciate it if you could keep your self-promotion activities in the Radix DLT Official Telegram channel to a brief introduction. Visit our #node-runners channel on <a id="" href="https://discord.gg/WkB2USt"><strong id="">Discord</strong></a> and you’ll find an amazing community of skilled node-runners.</p><p id="">It’s no easy feat to set up a brand new <a id="" href="https://learn.radixdlt.com/article/what-are-layer-1-l1-and-layer-2-l2-networks"><strong id="">layer 1 network</strong></a>, and we couldn’t have asked for more from the betanet validator community.
