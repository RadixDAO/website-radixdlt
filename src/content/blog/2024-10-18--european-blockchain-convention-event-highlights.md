We needed to understand that the assets are in the network level and not on the contract level</em> - <strong id="">Diego</strong></blockquote><p id="">Here is an insight into the five winning teams out of the 19 submissions received and what they’re building:</p><p id=""><strong id="">1st Place: Bondefi.xyz ($10K XRD)&nbsp;</strong></p><p id="">Bondefi.xyz is a launchpad designed to improve the token launch process. It features a pre-sale phase with custom pricing using bonding curves, guaranteed pre-sale goals with full refunds if not met (preventing the Token Generation Event), automatic DEX listing at TGE with locked liquidity, and immediate token utility. Trading fees from locked liquidity are redistributed through a "real-yield" staking program.</p><p id=""><strong id="">2nd Place: Radish ($5K XRD)</strong></p><p id="">Radish is a multi-collateralized lending platform where users can back loans with tokens like XRD, memecoins, and stablecoins. By collateralizing with multiple assets, Radish reduces liquidation risk through diversification. If one asset loses all value, the remaining assets maintain the loan-to-collateral ratio, preventing liquidation. This approach offers users greater stability and security in their lending.</p><p id=""><strong id="">3rd Place: PAWNFT ($3K XRD)&nbsp;</strong></p><p id="">PAWNFT is a decentralized peer-to-peer lending platform connecting NFT holders with liquidity providers. It allows NFT owners to access liquidity without selling their assets and eliminates the risk of automatic liquidations. By facilitating secure loans backed by NFTs, PAWNFT offers a valuable service to NFT holders seeking liquidity while retaining ownership of their digital assets.</p><p id=""><strong id="">4th Place: Solarix ($2K XRD)&nbsp;</strong></p><p id="">Solarix is a Web3 platform transforming the renewable energy market by enabling peer-to-peer energy sharing and investment in solar power through tokenized solar panels. It connects solar panel owners with excess energy, consumers seeking affordable clean energy, and investors interested in green assets. By tokenizing solar panels into NFTs, investors can support renewable projects and earn returns as energy is produced and sold, fostering a decentralized and transparent energy market.</p><p id=""><strong id="">5th Place: radix.meme ($1K XRD)</strong></p><p id="">radix.meme provides users an easy way to create and trade their own memecoins on Radix. Recognizing the popularity of memecoins, the platform offers a dedicated space for launching them.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Congratulations to the winning teams!
