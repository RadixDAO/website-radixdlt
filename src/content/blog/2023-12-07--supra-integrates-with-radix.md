<p id="">Radix Publishing is excited to announce that <a id="" href="https://supraoracles.com/">Supra</a>, a leader in oracle services, will be offering its oracle services on the Radix Network.</p><h3 id="">Supra’s Role in the Radix Ecosystem</h3><p id="">Supra will play a pivotal role in the continued growth of the Radix ecosystem, offering its <a href="https://supraoracles.com/academy/oracle-nomics-the-cost-of-trustless-data-validation/">"push" and "pull" oracle services</a> to deliver precise and reliable price data for more than 100 of the most popular crypto asset pairs. This will unlock a multitude of Web3 and DeFi use cases, including money markets, perpetuals, stablecoins, and prediction markets.&nbsp;</p><p id="">With Supra’s pull services, developers will be able to request specific pairs on demand, making data integration more flexible and tailored to the Radix ecosystem's needs.</p><p id="">What makes this integration even more exciting is Supra's integration of its Verifiable Random Function (VRF), enabling a wide range of use cases that rely on randomness, spanning Web3 gaming to metaverse experiences, NFTs, identity management, governance, and voting.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Piers Ridyard, CEO of RDX Works:</p><blockquote id="">“<em id="">Supra's reputation for excellence in the oracle space and their dedication to decentralization make them the ideal partner for Radix. We are excited to work together to enhance data services for the Radix Network and unlock new possibilities for the blockchain ecosystem.</em>”</blockquote><p id="">Joshua D. Tobkin, CEO and Co-founder of Supra:</p><blockquote id="">"<em id="">Collaborating with Radix is a significant step forward in our mission to empower the developer community with advanced oracle solutions. We are thrilled to bring our expertise to the Radix Network and contribute to the growth of Web3 and DeFi applications.</em>"</blockquote><h3 id="">About Supra</h3><p id="">Supra is dedicated to bridging the gap between traditional financial markets and the Web3 ecosystem. Their mission is to provide a powerful cross-chain oracle toolset to the developer community, enabling them to build, deploy, and manage data applications with improved performance, robustness, and agility.
