<p id="">The<a id="" href="https://incentives.radixdlt.com/"> Radix Rewards</a> program is all about celebrating and rewarding the community members who make the ecosystem thrive. From trading on DEXs to providing liquidity, you’ve been earning points for crucial on-ledger activities. Today, we are announcing a brand-new dimension to the Radix Rewards experience: <strong id="">Quests!</strong></p><p id="">Quests are a new way to earn Season Points (SP) by simply holding specific NFTs or other assets from across the Radix ecosystem in your wallet. Think of it as a digital scavenger hunt or a collector's bonus.</p><p id="">We’re initially rolling this out with RadQuest Radmorphs, as well as those who have<a id="" href="https://xrd.domains/"> XRD Domains</a>. From there, we will continue adding new assets, NFTs, and dApps to the quest system to reward holders of some of the most iconic NFTs on Radix.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">How Do Quests Work?</strong></h3><p id="">We've designed Quests to be simple, engaging, and rewarding. Here’s a step-by-step guide on how you can participate:</p><ol id=""><li><strong id="">Collect Eligible Tokens or NFTs:</strong> The first step is to acquire and hold tokens or NFTs from specific, Quest-enabled collections. We’ll be announcing new Quests and collections over time.</li><li><strong id="">Hold Them in Your Wallet:</strong> Ensure the eligible NFTs are in the same Radix account(s)that you use to connect to the Radix Rewards dashboard. The system will automatically detect them.</li><li><strong id="">Claim Your Points Weekly:</strong> Each week, you will need to visit the Radix Rewards dashboard and actively claim the points for the Quests you've completed. This is a simple, one-click process.</li><li><strong id="">Watch Your Score Grow:</strong> At the end of each weekly cycle, your claimed points will be added to your total Season Points (SP), boosting your position in the Radix Rewards program!</li></ol><p id=""><strong id="">Important Note:</strong> To ensure a fair and wide distribution of rewards, there will be a cap on the number of points you can earn per item type each week. This means holding more of a specific NFT will earn you more points, but only up to a certain limit.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The Inaugural Quests</strong></h3><p id="">To kick things off, we are launching with two foundational Quests that celebrate our early supporters and pioneers:</p><h4 id=""><strong id="">XRD Domain</strong></h4><ul id=""><li><strong id="">Mission: </strong>Hold one or more<a id="" href="https://xrd.domains/"> XRD Domains</a></li><li><strong id="">Reward:</strong> Earn 100 Season Points per week. Maximum one per user.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h4 id=""><strong id="">RadMorph&nbsp;</strong></h4><ul id=""><li><strong id="">Mission:</strong> Earn Radmorphs from<a id="" href="https://radquest.io"> Radquest</a>.</li><li><strong id="">Reward:</strong> Earn 10 Season Points per item per week. Maximum 10 items per user</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">A Glimpse into the Future</strong></h3><p id="">This is just the beginning! The Quests feature is built to be dynamic and expandable. In the future, we plan to introduce many more Quests featuring tokens and NFTs from a wide range of projects and dApps.</p><p id="">Imagine earning points for holding:</p><ul id=""><li>Dev badges from innovative projects.</li><li>NFTs from your favorite Radix-based games.</li><li>Special edition art from top-tier collections.</li><li>Participation trophies from community events.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">The possibilities are endless, and we can't wait to collaborate with the incredible builders across the ecosystem to bring you more exciting Quests.</p><p id="">GL, HF!</p>
