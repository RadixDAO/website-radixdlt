<p id="">The Milestone Rewards, recently announced as part of the Radix <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/25m-xrd-1m-ecosystem-fund-to-support-dapp-builders"><strong id="">Ecosystem Fund</strong></a>, are now officially live, with this initial set of rewards being offered to the first dApps who reached $1M Total Value Locked (TVL) on the Babylon Mainnet. TVL is a key metric in decentralized finance (DeFi) which assesses the health, security, and adoption of a Layer 1 protocol and its ecosystem.&nbsp;</p><p id="">The first Milestone Rewards are attributed to:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><a id="" href="https://www.caviarnine.com/"><strong id="">CaviarNine</strong></a>, the leading decentralized exchange on Radix, offers a smart trade aggregator, concentrated liquidity, LSU pool, and more!</li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://ociswap.com/"><strong id="">Ociswap</strong></a>, another leading decentralized exchange, focused on providing a comprehensive and user-friendly DeFi experience.</li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://defiplaza.net/"><strong id="">DefiPlaza</strong></a>, Built on top of Radix and Ethereum, DefiPlaza is an innovative DEX that is designed to reduce the risk of Impermanent Loss. </li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://app.weft.finance/home"><strong id="">Weft Finance</strong></a>, a lending and borrowing application on Radix.</li></ul><p id=""><br>Each team receives a $10,000 reward paid in XRD. Apply now for the <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radix-booster-grants-apply-now"><strong id="">Radix Booster Grants</strong></a>, another key initiative of the $25M Ecosystem Fund, and receive up to $15,000 in XRD to support the development of your dApp on the Radix network.</p>
