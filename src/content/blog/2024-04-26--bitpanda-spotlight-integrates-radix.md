<h3 id=""><strong id="">Bitpanda Spotlight Welcomes Radix</strong></h3><p id="">Bitpanda, a leading investment platform based in Austria, has announced the upcoming integration of Radix through its <a id="" href="https://support.bitpanda.com/hc/en-us/articles/7826669925404-What-is-Bitpanda-Spotlight">Bitpanda Spotlight </a>program. Bitpanda Spotlight showcases innovative projects, making Radix a notable inclusion, given its potential and alignment with Bitpanda's stringent selection criteria.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">What This Means for Radix Users</strong></h3><p id="">Having Bitpanda Spotlight integrate Radix opens significant avenues for Radix by exposing it to a broad audience of over four million users across more than <a id="" href="https://support.bitpanda.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013898239-Countries-supported-for-verification">40 countries</a> in Europe and the Middle East. Through the Spotlight Program, Radix is featured as a promising Layer 1 network, encouraging them to explore the unique capabilities and innovations within the Radix Ecosystem. This integration provides an excellent opportunity to amplify Radix’s presence in a new user base, broaden the community, and affirm the commitment to innovation and regulatory compliance in the Web3 Wild West, and compliments the wider #Breakout2024 activities.&nbsp;</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Benefits for Bitpanda's BEST Holders</strong></h3><p id="">Bitpanda’s BEST (Bitpanda Ecosystem Token) holders have additional opportunities thanks to this integration.
