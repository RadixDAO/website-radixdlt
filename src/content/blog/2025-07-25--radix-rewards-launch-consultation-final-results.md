<p id="">The token holder consultation on the <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/token-holder-consultation-radix-rewards-launch">Radix Rewards launch timing</a> has now concluded.</p><p id="">The results are clear: 467 million XRD across 707 accounts participated, with <strong id="">77.44% signaling a preference to launch Radix Rewards once the Hyperlane integration is live</strong>.&nbsp;Account voting can be found <a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JxvmYEHvnntaCRpTptKU01zIrJfqIBhe0Qw91MPNgms/edit?usp=sharing">here</a>.</p><p id="">This outcome verifies that the majority token weight is aligned in launching Radix Rewards alongside permissionless bridging to 100+ chains to maximize Day 1 impact and ecosystem growth.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">What This Means</strong></h2><p id="">The Radix community has chosen substance over speed. By aligning the incentives program with Hyperlane's launch, we have the opportunity to create the foundation for genuine multi-chain liquidity and adoption from the moment we go live.</p><p id="">Both Hyperlane integration and Radix Rewards development remain <strong id="">firmly on track</strong>. Both teams are executing at full speed toward the targeted timeline.</p><p id="">However, we've heard the community loud and clear on timing expectations. If anything threatens to push the launch beyond the end of August, we will immediately proceed with the Radix Rewards rollout. The community's patience has limits, and we respect that.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Moving Forward</strong></h2><p id="">This consultation once again demonstrates the maturity of the Radix ecosystem and community with token holders engaging in nuanced strategic discussion.
