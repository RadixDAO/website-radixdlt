We are only just getting started.<br></p><h3 id="">Strategy &amp; Marketing</h3><h5 id="">Adam Simmons, Head of Strategy - Radix DLT</h5><p id="">It’s incredible to think that this time next week, the Olympia Mainnet will be up and running!&nbsp;</p><p id="">Over the next few days, you will all start seeing our marketing campaigns ramping up, including social advertising, influencer marketing, and PR - all with a focus on this major milestone for Radix, as well as the new <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/making-xrd-a-cross-chain-token-with-exrd-and-instabridge">Instapass and Instabridge products</a> and the start of <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/radix-staking-and-incentive-rewards-guide">staking</a> with <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/radix-staking-and-incentive-rewards-guide">300m XRD annual network emissions</a>.&nbsp;</p><p id="">It’s going to be a busy few weeks, but for now, let’s look at the last two weeks:</p><ul id=""><li id="">We provided additional details about the Olympia mainnet <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/radix-mainnet-launch-timeline">launch timeline</a>, starting on June 30th. The blog post explains it in detail; however, the key thing to note is there is no rush to start swapping eXRD for XRD, staking, connecting nodes, or registering validators during the bootstrapping period. During this period, everyone will be equally “first in line” when the bootstrapping epoch ends and normal epochs begin.&nbsp;</li><li id="">For those wanting to use <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/making-xrd-a-cross-chain-token-with-exrd-and-instabridge">Instapass and Instabridge</a> to convert eXRD to XRD, the first step is to register with Instapass when it goes live on June 30th. Instabridge will then go live on July 7th to facilitate converting eXRD to XRD. We are working with partners and exchanges, such as Bitfinex, who will also be offering the ability to swap between eXRD and XRD.
