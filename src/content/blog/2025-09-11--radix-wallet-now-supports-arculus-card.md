<h1 id=""><strong id="">Radix Wallet Now Supports Arculus Card&nbsp;</strong></h1><p id="">We’re excited to announce that Radix Wallet now supports the <strong id="">Arculus Card</strong>, which makes secure account management and professional-grade protection easier than ever. This marks the second step in our <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/arculus-partners-with-radix-to-support-mfa">partnership with Arculus</a>, following the earlier listing of XRD in the Arculus Wallet app.</p><p id="">With this integration, Radix users can now:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Create and manage <strong id="">accounts and personas.<br></strong></li><li id=""><strong id="">Sign transactions</strong> securely with just a tap.<br></li><li id=""><strong id="">Prove ownership</strong> of accounts with confidence.<br></li><li id="">Use the card as a <strong id="">recovery factor.</strong><br><br></li></ul><h3 id=""><strong id="">Arculus Card Advantages for Radix Users</strong></h3><p id="">Using the Arculus Card as your Radix Wallet signing device combines the proven security of hardware protection with the convenience of a tap. Here’s why it’s a great choice:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Private keys stay on the card,</strong> they never leave the secure hardware.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Increased safety against attacks</strong>: even if your phone is compromised, the transaction won’t go through unless you tap the card.</li><li id=""><strong id="">One-tap signing</strong>: no cables, PINs, or buttons.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Fits in a normal wallet</strong> or purse, unlike bulkier USB devices.</li><li id=""><strong id="">No charging required</strong>: the card uses NFC, so it never needs to be plugged in or powered up.<strong id="">‍</strong></li><li id=""><strong id="">Works with any NFC-enabled phone</strong>, no dependency on USB port type.<strong id="">‍</strong></li><li id=""><strong id="">Travel-friendly</strong>: it’s durable, waterproof, and tamper-resistant.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Make Sure You’re Using a Compatible Arculus Card</strong></h3><p id="">To use your Arculus Card with the Radix Wallet, you’ll need to make sure you have a <strong id="">compatible version of the card firmware</strong>. The Radix Wallet requires <strong id="">Arculus Card version 2.2.7.6 or newer</strong>. You can verify its version number in the <strong id="">Arculus Wallet App</strong> settings before pairing it with the Radix Wallet.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">How to Add Your Arculus Card to Radix Wallet</strong></h3><ol id=""><li id="">In Radix Wallet, go to <strong id=""><em id="">Security Factors</em></strong> and select <strong id=""><em id="">Arculus Card</em></strong>.<br></li><li id="">Tap <strong id=""><em id="">Add Arculus Card</em></strong>.<br></li><li id="">Set up your card following these steps:<br><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id=""><em id="">Seed Phrase Configuration</em></strong>: Choose whether to create a new seed phrase or import the one already configured in your Arculus Wallet app.<br></li><li id=""><strong id=""><em id="">Card PIN</em></strong>: Set up a secure PIN for your card.<br></li><li id=""><strong id=""><em id="">Card Name</em></strong>: Give your card a name so you can easily identify it later.</li></ul></li></ol><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:2048px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="2048px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/68c2c64e2c39dee0d7473af0_69a02a8c.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p>‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Managing Your Arculus Card&nbsp;</strong></h3><p id="">Once your Arculus Card has been added, it will appear in your <strong id=""><em id="">Security Factors</em></strong> list. From there, you can manage your card with the following options:</p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Change Name</strong> – Update the card’s display name at any time.<br></li><li id=""><strong id="">Change PIN</strong> – Set a new PIN whenever you like.<br></li><li id=""><strong id="">Forgot PIN</strong> – If you’ve forgotten your PIN, you can reset it by tapping on <strong id=""><em id="">Forgot PIN</em></strong>.&nbsp;</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><p id="">After your Arculus Card is added to the Radix Wallet, you can start using it right away. Whenever you create a new account or persona, simply select the card when prompted, and it will serve as the signing factor for that account.</p><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:2048px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="2048px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/68c2c64e2c39dee0d7473aed_29f835b1.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Radix Wallet 1.17.0 for Arculus Card Support</strong></h3><p id="">Update your <a id="" href="https://wallet.radixdlt.com/">Radix Wallet</a> to version 1.17.0 today to unlock Arculus Card support. With the latest release, you can start using your Arculus Card for secure signing and recovery in just a tap.</p>
