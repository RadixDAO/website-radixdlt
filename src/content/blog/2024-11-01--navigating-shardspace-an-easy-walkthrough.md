<p id="">ShardSpace is an all-in-one digital asset management platform for the Radix Ecosystem, streamlining token, NFT, staking, and trading activities. With a user-friendly drag-and-drop interface, ShardSpace includes features like a Shopping Cart for batch transactions and an Address Book integrated with XRD Domains for easy contact management and transactions.&nbsp;</p><p id="">The Token Manager allows developers to easily create and manage tokens, conduct airdrops, and monitor token performance. Project owners also benefit from robust tools to mint, burn, recall, update token metadata, and transfer tokens, all in one streamlined platform.<br><br>ShardSpace streamlines asset management for both everyday users and developers.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">How To Use ShardSpace</strong></h2><p id="">Here are some key features that help users manage assets and accounts, navigate the Radix ecosystem, and delve into the more developer-focused features.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Three things you’ll need:</strong>&nbsp;</p><ol id=""><li id="">If you haven’t already, you’ll need to download the <a id="" href="https://wallet.radixdlt.com/">Radix Wallet<br><br></a></li><li id="">Have enough XRD in your Radix Wallet to conduct simple transactions and pay fees, $10 should do it; you<a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/token"> can buy XRD quite easily</a>.<br><br></li><li id="">Have your mobile device and/or laptop/PC ready. On ShardSpace, you can utilize Radix Connect for Mobile, which means you can use the platform on a mobile web browser without needing a desktop device.</li></ol><p id="">The Portfolio page is your main dashboard, where you can view all resource types across your accounts. Managing your assets is as easy as drag and drop.</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Easy Asset Transfers</strong></h2><p id=""><strong id="">Here’s how you can instantly transfer assets across your accounts:</strong></p><ul id=""><li id="">Go to the <a id="" href="https://launch.shardspace.app/portfolio/accounts">Portfolio</a> page.<br><br></li><li id="">Simply click on a token icon and drag it to another account in your list.<br><br></li><li id="">A pop-up will prompt you to specify the number of tokens to move.<br><br></li><li id="">Choose between ‘Quick Move’ or ‘Add to Cart’ for batch transfers.<br><br></li><li id="">Finally, approve the transaction in your Radix Wallet.<br>‍</li></ul><p id="">This feature isn’t just for tokens—it works for NFTs, Domain Badges, and other resources, making portfolio management smooth and intuitive.<br>‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:675px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="675px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/672383696d80d03c6ab32988_AD_4nXf8_Ag_RDinMmYCi1-NbfecZbtvWt-jOchcNYIhwv4EpuvCOXqUttZN2Zq7hk1m1_IYX5J93ZQXvSd_e7H5tnd8eH5xaxd1U6PDsZCKdXf5pfMHuOHAdGv7Kf3Ad_UDsBuj0bIw-Q-PlnrDnk_dBPpyTcy.png" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"></div></figure><h2 id=""><strong id="">Staking</strong></h2><p id="">The Portfolio page also includes a <a id="" href="https://launch.shardspace.app/portfolio/staking">Staking tab</a> to manage your staking positions across all accounts and Validators.
