<p id="">RDX Holdings and the Radix Foundation are pleased to announce the selection of the Radix Endowment investment manager as <a id="" href="https://www.brevanhoward.com/brevanhoward-digital/">Brevan Howard Digital</a>, concluding the selection process in line with the previous blog post: <a href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/the-radix-endowment-fund">The Radix Endowment Fund</a></p><p id="">Brevan Howard Digital is the digital asset investment platform of <a id="" href="https://www.brevanhoward.com/">Brevan Howard</a>, one of the world’s largest hedge funds, managing around $34 billion. Brevan Howard Digital recently launched its BH Digital Solutions business to offer companies crypto treasury management, including services to help with the management of liquidity, risk reduction and yield generation.</p><p id="">The XRD-denominated contributions have been transferred to BH Digital Solutions’ SPC accounts at <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/copper-integrates-radix-into-institutional-custody-services">Copper Custody</a>.&nbsp;</p><p id="">As a regulated and well reputed firm, Brevan Howard Digital has been selected as the investment manager to implement the core strategies that have been prioritized by the various boards responsible for the fiduciary management and control of the relevant Radix assets.</p>
