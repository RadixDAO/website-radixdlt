<p id="">After the successful <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/radix-betanet-is-live-quickstart-guide">Radix Betanet</a> launch, the team has been charging forward in high spirits. Squashing bugs, bringing in new hires, delivering updates, you name it.
