<h4 id=""><strong id="">Overview (Adam Simmons, CSO)</strong></h4><p id="">I’m filling in for Piers in this Radix Report, and want to use the column inches to give a bit more of a look to the future, and what we’re building up to.&nbsp;</p><p id="">As Jeremy mentions below, the marketing team is currently focused on increasing the amount of word-of-mouth sharing around Radix.&nbsp;</p><p id="">A big ethos behind this is what I like to think of as 1-degree of separation marketing; it’s hard to reach (and get mindshare from) the most influential/impactful people directly. Afterall, people are bombarded with bold claims 24/7, so naturally, we filter. Yet, when someone trusted tells us we should take a closer look at something, we do.&nbsp; This is where word of mouth becomes so powerful.&nbsp;</p><p id="">In September we have some major activities, such as attending <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/spreading-the-radix-revelation-at-messari-mainnet-new-york">Messari Mainnet</a> &amp; <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/bringing-the-radixrevelation-to-token2049-singapore">Token 2049</a>, that help drive awareness. But word of mouth isn’t just about awareness, it’s also about ensuring people are confident in lending Radix some of their “social capital” - which is why milestones such as the Babylon Alphanet, or getting published regularly in press outlets, are so important.&nbsp;</p><p id="">However, what we cannot forget is that gaining credibility isn’t just down to the technology, but what people do with it. This is why it’s so important to celebrate the continued success Scrypto developers are having, such as the entries into the latest <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/scrypto-portfolio-challenge-results">Scrypto challenge</a>, even in this early stage.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Looking further forward, Q4 will see a ramp up in activity as we prepare for the Babylon Betanet. This will see an increased focus on getting Radix into the consciousness of the broader industry, and users, as we begin to seed the excitement for Babylon and the opportunity the new Radix DeFi ecosystem going live in 2023 will present.</p><p id="">As each day passes, Babylon edges closer. Momentum grows. But remember, we are all here to realize the same vision, a radically better global financial system.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Fulfilling that vision won’t be easy, there will be bumps, but if we all work together to help spread the word, welcome those who join us, support and celebrate the success of those who build in the Radix ecosystem, I know we will look back proudly knowing we were part of such a revolutionary moment and movement.</p><p id="">‍</p><h4 id=""><strong id="">Strategy &amp;
