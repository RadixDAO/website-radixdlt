<p id="">As the first season of Radix Rewards has now drawn to a close, we’ve been busy calculating everyone's points and share of the rewards pool. We’re ahead of schedule on this, which means we’re going to be kicking off the distribution shortly!</p><p id="">On <strong id="">February 3rd, 2026, at approximately 16:00 UTC</strong>, the vesting for Radix Rewards Season 0 and Season 1 goes live. This release represents a total pool of <strong id="">114,347,194.845748 XRD</strong>, including the Season 0 bonus.</p><p id="">Following the community consultation, we have implemented a structured vesting schedule to ensure a stable transition for the ecosystem. The process is split into two steps:</p><ol id=""><li id=""><strong id="">Claiming: </strong>You can claim 100% of your pool units to your wallet immediately. This is your total share of the reward pool.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Redeeming:</strong> <strong id="">20%</strong> of your XRD is available for immediate redemption and the remaining <strong id="">80%</strong> vests linearly over a <strong id="">7-day period</strong>.</li></ol><p id="">‍</p><p id=""><strong id="">The Diamond Hands Bonus</strong></p><p id="">If a participant decides to exit their position fully before the 7-day vest is complete, they don't just leave, they leave a "tip" to those who remain.</p><p id="">Redeeming your pool units before the vesting completes means your unvested XRD is <strong id="">distributed proportionally</strong> to those who remain in the pool.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="">A Note on Privacy</h3><p id="">In an open ledger environment, privacy is a choice. We advise that you claim your rewards across <strong id="">multiple accounts using non-equal percentages</strong>. Don't make it easy for observers to map your total holdings. Direct your units to separate accounts to maintain a clean, anonymous on-chain footprint.</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Next Steps</strong></h3><p id="">The dashboard is ready. Connect your wallet, verify your units, and manage your redemption starting tomorrow at 16:00 UTC.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Access the Rewards Portal:</strong><a href="https://incentives.radixdlt.com/"> https://incentives.radixdlt.com/</a></p>
