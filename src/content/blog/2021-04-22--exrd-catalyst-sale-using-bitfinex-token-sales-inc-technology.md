<p id="">Radix Tokens (Jersey) Limited is pleased to announce that they will be selling a small amount of eXRD using the Bitfinex Token Sales Inc. Catalyst Sale planform on April 27th, 2021, starting at 11:00AM UTC. </p><p id="">The purpose of this Catalyst sale is to promote the distribution of stake and further the awareness of Radix to the users of the Catalyst sale platform and therefore, only 1,400,000 eXRD will be available in the Catalyst sale. In the event that the sale is oversubscribed, eXRD will be distributed proportionally to all participants in the Catalyst Sale.eXRD sold during the Catalyst sale will be based on the spot price at the time of the sale, with buyers receiving a bonus 40% tokens a few weeks after the sale is closed.</p><p id="">To participate in the Catalyst Sale, users will need to complete KYC verification prior to the sale opening. Verification starts at 11:00AM UTC on April 21st, and can be accessed via the eXRD sale page on the Catalyst Sale platform <a id="" href="https://tokensales.bitfinex.com/token-sale/607daee3b3316599efb60115/Radix">here</a>.</p><p id="">Conducting a Catalyst Sale with Bitfinex Token Sales is a milestone moment and another step forward for eXRD, growing the potential user base of the Radix network in the lead up to the Olympia release of the Radix Public Network in Q2’2021.Full instructions on how to purchase tokens using the Bitfinex Token Sales platform can be found <a id="" href="https://tokensales.bitfinex.com/help/purchase">here</a>.</p>
