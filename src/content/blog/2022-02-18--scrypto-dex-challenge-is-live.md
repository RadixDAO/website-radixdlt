<p id="">Scrypto, the smart contract language of Radix, was released in “early access” form two months ago and the feedback we have received from the community has convinced us that Scrypto’s asset-oriented programming style is the game changer for decentralized applications that we believed it would be. We often hear how easy it is to get started and to build things that would be considered complex or risky on other platforms like Ethereum.&nbsp;</p><p id="">To encourage developers to get started in Scrypto, and to let community members show off their skills, today we are launching a Scrypto competition – with prizes!&nbsp;</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">The Challenge</strong></h3><p id="">Starting today, you will have four weeks to create a blueprint package for an <strong id="">exchange dApp</strong> in Scrypto. You could build your own version of Uniswap, an order book exchange, or even a peer-to-peer trading platform like AirSwap. You can build anything, as long as it is related to allowing exchange between different assets.</p><p id="">Obviously front-end UI development is not yet practical using the Alexandria simulator, so this will be purely a competition of blueprints, tested and demonstrated through transaction manifests (one of the big new <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/scrypto-v0-3-released">Scrypto v0.3 features!</a>)</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Prizes</strong></h3><p id="">In this competition, everyone will walk away with something for participating. Everyone who submits an entry to the challenge will be put on a list to receive a special NFT after Babylon’s release proving that you were one of the first Scrypto developers. Maybe you can even use that NFT to show your future DLT employer how early you were in the Radix ecosystem.</p><p id="">The top three entries will receive special XRD token prizes. We are offering $1000 in XRD for the best example,&nbsp; $600 in XRD to the second and $400 in XRD to the third, along with NFT medals (again to be provided after Babylon’s release).&nbsp;</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">How we will judge the examples</strong></h3><p id="">After the close of the submission deadline, the Scrypto team will take some time to judge the examples and decide on the winners. We will base our decision on a combination of:</p><ul id=""><li id="">Quality and asset-orientedness of your code. Refer to our <a id="" href="https://docs.radixdlt.com/main/scrypto/design-patterns/user-badge-pattern.html">design patterns page</a>.</li><li id="">Breadth of functionality</li><li id="">Creativity of the concept</li><li id="">Quality of the documentation and comments in the code for others who wish to use the blueprint</li><li id="">Usage of the <a id="" href="https://docs.radixdlt.com/main/scrypto/transaction-manifest/intro.html">transaction manifest</a> to show composability and allow easier testing</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">How to participate</strong></h3><p id="">Here are the steps to register and submit your entry:</p><ol id=""><li id=""><a id="" href="https://developers.radixdlt.com/devprogram">Register for the Developer Program</a> with your GitHub account</li><li id="">Install the <a id="" href="https://github.com/radixdlt/radixdlt-scrypto">Scrypto toolchain</a></li><li id="">Fork <a id="" href="https://github.com/radixdlt/scrypto-challenges">this repository</a></li><li id="">Clone the forked repository on your local environment</li><li id="">Start a new scrypto project with `scrypto new-package`</li><li id=""><strong id="">Before the deadline on March 18</strong>, commit and push your project folder and create a pull request <a id="" href="https://github.com/radixdlt/scrypto-challenge-exchanges/pulls">here</a></li></ol><h3 id=""><strong id="">Resources</strong></h3><p id="">Looking for inspiration? You might consider these resources to get a grasp on some existing applications related to exchanges.&nbsp;</p><ul id=""><li id=""><a id="" href="https://www.coindesk.com/learn/2021/08/20/what-is-an-automated-market-maker">What is an automated market maker ?</a></li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/post/why-defi-is-the-future-of-exchanges-and-trading">Why DeFi is the future of exchanges and trading by RDX Works</a></li><li id=""><a id="" href="https://github.com/radixdlt/radixdlt-scrypto/blob/main/examples/defi/radiswap/src/lib.rs">Radiswap Scrypto example</a></li></ul><ul id=""><li id="">Automated Market Makers: <a id="" href="https://docs.uniswap.org/protocol/V2/concepts/protocol-overview/how-uniswap-works">Uniswap</a>, <a id="" href="https://www.gemini.com/cryptopedia/curve-finance-liquidity-provider-dao#section-composability-incentivizing-liquidity-providers">Curve</a>, <a id="" href="https://docs.balancer.fi/products/balancer-pools/weighted-pools">Balancer</a></li><li id="">Peer to peer trading: <a id="" href="https://about.airswap.io/">AirSwap</a></li><li id="">Aggregators: <a id="" href="https://www.gemini.com/cryptopedia/what-is-kyber-network">KyberSwap</a>, <a id="" href="https://docs.1inch.io/docs/aggregation-protocol/introduction/">1inch</a></li></ul><p id="">‍</p><h3 id=""><strong id="">Details of the challenge</strong></h3><ul id=""><li id="">To enter, you must be a registered member of the <a id="" href="https://developers.radixdlt.com/devprogram">Radix Developer Program</a>.</li><li id="">Your entry must be built with Scrypto v0.3.</li><li id="">You can submit multiple examples.</li><li id="">The competition starts on 18th February 2022 12:00 UTC. Winners will be required to disclose their name, DOB, and country of residence to receive any XRD prizes.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p>
