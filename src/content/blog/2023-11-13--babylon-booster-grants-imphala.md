<figure class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="1600px"><div><img src="/assets/655cc77d6a9d2a91c6bb1d59_Imphala.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure><p>The &nbsp;<a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/booster-program-participants-the-momentum-builds" target="_blank"><strong id="">Babylon Booster Grant</strong></a> has been successfully awarded to Impahla. </p><h3 id=""><strong id="">What is Impahla?&nbsp;</strong></h3><p id=""><a id="" href="https://impahla.io/">Impahla</a> is designed to streamline the creation, purchase, and management of NFTs. This platform <a id="" href="https://medium.com/@impahla/what-problems-does-impahla-solve-3561e478693a">differs from conventional marketplaces</a> by offering an integrated ecosystem for users to engage with digital assets on the Radix network efficiently and securely.</p><p id="">Impahla attracted 77 creators and 48 collections in the first two days and has actively incorporated community feedback to improve the platform. The team swiftly executed 4 out of 10 planned updates to better align with user suggestions, manifesting a user-centric approach and agile response to their needs.</p><p id="">Looking ahead, Impahla aims to introduce five new high-quality collections each month to maintain a vibrant and engaging marketplace. This approach is intended to <a id="" href="https://medium.com/@impahla/a-discussion-of-nft-marketplaces-for-the-impahla-platform-5417583e02a6">keep Impahla competitive</a> within the Radix NFT space.</p><p id="">In terms of technological innovation, Impahla is exploring decentralized storage options, such as IPFS, to store NFTs, which will further contribute to the assets' longevity and security while aligning with the decentralized ethos for crypto.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Overall, Impahla strives to build a reliable and adaptable Radix NFT marketplace, offering an intuitive interface and a comprehensive range of features grounded in a clear vision and a commitment to its users.&nbsp;</p><p id="">The objective is straightforward: to make Impahla the go-to place for NFT creators and collectors on Radix. Their roadmap reflects a deep dedication to innovation and the desire to set new benchmarks in the NFT market, focusing on user experience, and security whilst adhering to regulatory standards.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Here's what's on the near horizon for users:</strong></p><ul id=""><li id=""><strong id="">New Fee Structure:</strong> Impahla is introducing a 5% fee on initial NFT sales and a 2.5% fee on secondary sales</li><li id=""><strong id="">Enhanced Transparency: </strong>The ledger will store more detailed information on NFT transactions, ensuring a higher degree of transparency.</li><li id=""><strong id="">IPFS Integration:</strong> For improved security and durability, Impahla is going to offer IPFS as an option for NFT storage.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Market Analytics:</strong> Tracking of sales volume for all NFTs will be implemented, offering users insightful market data.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Royalty Fees dApp Mechanism:</strong> A new system to manage Royalty fees on the dApp level is under development.</li><li id=""><strong id="">Strategic Partnerships:</strong> Plans to partner with platforms like <a id="" href="https://artizyou.com/">Artizyou</a> for digital rights management are underway.</li><li id=""><strong id="">DeFi Integration: </strong>Impahla is exploring DeFi opportunities, including lending applications that allow NFTs as collateral.</li></ul><p id="">‍</p><figure id="" class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:2820px" data-rt-type="image" data-rt-align="fullwidth" data-rt-max-width="2820px"><div id=""><img id="" alt="" src="/assets/65524c2d9f1b59ecc962c9ae_Screenshot_2023-11-09_at_21.48.08-1.jpg" width="auto" height="auto" loading="lazy"></div></figure><h3 id=""><strong id="">The Radix Advantage: A Founder's Perspective</strong></h3><p id="">The founders of Impahla, leaders in the Radix NFT scene, have a clear goal: to make trading digital assets simple and secure.
