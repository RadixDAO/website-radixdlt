This allows users to enjoy a seamless, DeFi-native experience on Radix that they know and love.</blockquote><p id="">‍</p><p id="">In a recent video interview, Piers Ridyard, CEO of RDX Works and Head Protocol Architect, Josh Primero explore Anthic and its integration of Flash Liquidity. Piers and Josh talk through how Anthic works, highlighting how the system uses intents. They explain how Anthic will enhance liquidity on the Network and deliver a groundbreaking user trading experience to the Radix platform and Radix users.</p><p id="">Josh talks through the revolutionary concept of Flash Liquidity which allows market makers to offer real-time, deep liquidity without locking up capital until a trade is confirmed. This model not only enhances capital efficiency but also minimizes slippage and spreads, aligning with the needs of both retail traders and institutional participants.&nbsp;</p><p id="">As detailed in earlier blog posts, <a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/flash-liquidity-a-game-changer-for-radix-defi">Flash Liquidity</a> is the key to uniting scattered liquidity across various platforms, bringing a seamless, centralized exchange-like efficiency to Radix’s DeFi ecosystem. The real-world impact?&nbsp; For traders, Anthic means access to global liquidity of any top crypto asset - from BTC, ETH, and SOL through to DOGE, LINK, and HBAR - without the usual barriers, enabling instant and competitively priced trades. For market makers, it’s an opportunity to operate more dynamically, adapting to market conditions without the constraints of traditional liquidity provisions.</p><p id="">Anthic enables a radically better user experience by mitigating the cumbersome process of managing multiple wallets and bridging assets across chains to access deep liquidity. It creates a unified venue where trading is as easy as trading on a centralized platform but with the heightened security and transparency of DeFi.</p><p id=""><strong id="">Why Watch the Interview?</strong> The interview provides an in-depth look at how Anthic leverages Flash Liquidity to solve one of DeFi's most pressing challenges: <strong id="">fragmented liquidity</strong>.</p><p id="">So, if you haven’t fully grasped what Anthic brings to the Radix ecosystem, this is a must-watch.
