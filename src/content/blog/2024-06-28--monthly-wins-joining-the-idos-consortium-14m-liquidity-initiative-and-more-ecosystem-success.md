<p id="">Hello, Radvocates! <br><br>It's been an exciting month again for Radix and the community as Radvocate numbers have grown to over 250,000 on X (Twitter), 36,000 on Telegram, and 27,000 on Discord. Many new folks are getting their Radix Revelation, which is great news! Not only that but Radix Wallet iOS and Android downloads have officially surpassed 80,000.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Other highlights include the launch of SuperStaking and the recent increase in Token Trek users to 33,208 with over 64,268 quests completed as well as new projects and quests being added daily.&nbsp;</p><p id="">Here's an overview of the past month 👇</p><h2 id=""><strong id="">Latest Updates</strong></h2><p id=""><a id="" href="https://www.radixdlt.com/blog/radix-joins-idos-consortium">Radix Joins idOS Consortium:</a> Radix is joining the idOS Consortium, a decentralized identity network designed for storing and managing access to decentralized identities.
