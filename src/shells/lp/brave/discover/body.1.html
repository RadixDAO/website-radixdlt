Where can I buy?

XRD is Radix’s native token. You can purchase XRD on the Bitfinex exchange. eXRD is the wrapped ERC-20 version of XRD, issued on the Ethereum network. eXRD is listed on Kucoin , Gate.io , Uniswap , and other exchanges. eXRD can be exchanged 1:1 with XRD.
