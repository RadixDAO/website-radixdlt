Transaction Manifest

Radix transactions give you complete visibility and control before you sign, with a "manifest" of meaningful instructions the Radix Wallet can summarize. Unlike other networks, Radix transactions directly describe the movements of your assets, and you can add guarantees for the desired outcome.

With the Radix Wallet, you can sign transactions with confidence, knowing that it's showing you exactly what will happen to your assets.

With Radix, users get transparent, secure, and user friendly transactions that are ready for mass adoption.

Smart Accounts

To make a wallet experience that’s safe, fully decentralized, and as easy as using a typical banking app, Radix had to rethink how accounts on a blockchain work. They’re called Smart Accounts.

Unlike regular blockchain accounts, Smart Accounts on Radix aren’t just an address. They’re functional containers for assets that have multi-signature signing and multi-factor recovery built right in – on the network. This means that the Radix Wallet can rely on those security features, but if you lose your phone, the smart account lets you recover access without a seed phrase and without trusting a centralized service.

Personas

Web 3.0 demands a better login solution than Web 2.0. Passwords are a hassle and a security risk, and relying on giants like Apple, Google, and Microsoft for your login secrets isn't ideal.

Personas are like Smart Accounts for identity. They offer the same on-ledger multifactor security as Smart Accounts, but instead of holding tokens, they control access to your login.

With Personas, the Radix Wallet delivers passwordless, personalized logins that are secure, decentralized, and anonymous.
