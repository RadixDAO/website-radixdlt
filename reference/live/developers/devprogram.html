Radix Developer Program

Join the community and create history by revolutionizing global finance

Scrypto is going to change the way we build DeFi apps

Engineered with a focus on security and simplicity, Scrypto lets you leave all the boring parts behind and do what you really love - Build DeFi apps.

To get you started on this journey, we have launched the Radix Developer Program, which will guide you, support you, and reward you while building the next DeFi app.

Why you should join Radix

Learn

Gain an understanding of the opportunity-rich world of DeFi. Discover and share programming best practices as you level up your Scrypto skills. Whether you are new to the DeFi ecosystem or have been building apps on other platforms, we’ve got you covered.

Earn Rewards

Join a global community of excited developers to learn, collaborate and build amazing stuff. Whether you have a brilliant idea but don’t know how to go about it or are stuck with code that is not compiling, our community is here to help and support you. Wanna meetup? Meet and greet fellow developers and discuss everything DeFi at our exclusive developer events.

Connect

Get recognized for your contributions. Earn points and showcase your Scrypto prowess to the world. While you are at it, get some XRD in your wallet or snatch a cool Radix swag. Every contribution counts.

Exclusive Access

Get priority access to exclusive developer events and programs. Be the first to hear about what is happening in the developer community and get a headstart.
